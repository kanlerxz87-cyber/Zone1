A correction to how this record argued its own case, dated 2026-11-17.

Rows above and flags around them cite the absence of a visible tag= parameter on an observed landing URL beside their caution about attribution. Two official help nodes read on this date narrow what that can carry. Amazon states plainly that a tracking ID does not need to appear in the click-through URL for a sale to be credited, because the session is tagged on its side S41 — so a clean landing URL is a null observation, not a negative one, and it cannot be used as evidence about attribution in either direction. What stays unprovable from outside the account is the positive: that the selected ID is listed, and that a click through a published link appears under it. S41's sibling node names where that is read — the Tracking ID Summary Report — together with the screen that creates IDs and the limit of 100 tracking IDs per account S42, which is also what makes a per-channel sub-tag scheme a cheap option rather than an exotic one. The per-row associates_link_test_* fields are the right place for that result, and nothing on this page will accept a URL inspection as a substitute for it. Historical rows are left as written: they are dated records of what those passes observed, and the reasoning is corrected here instead of edited backwards.
