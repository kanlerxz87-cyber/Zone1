2 · Editorial policy (why “data-safe”)

  • No invented facts. A product claim is written only when it appears on the product's official Amazon page and is recorded with its source. (FTC: “You can't talk about your experience with a product you haven't tried” — Disclosures 101.)
  • Blank beats wrong. Claim slots show an explicit “pending verification” marker rather than filler text.
  • Disclosure first. Every asset carries “As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases.” near the top and in the footer (Operating Agreement §5).
  • Proper link format. CTAs use tagged Special Links only (Operating Agreement §1); until a tag exists, CTAs are visibly disabled — never silently misdirected.

4 · Activation plan (once valid ASINs + tag arrive)

  1. Fill each claim slot from the product's official page (title, price via compliant source, rating, review count, category) with per-claim source links.
