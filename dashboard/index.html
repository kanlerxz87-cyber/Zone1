What is needed to activate product marketing

  1. Three valid 10-character ASINs (or full amazon.com/dp/… product-page URLs) for the intended products.
  2. Your Amazon Associates tracking tag (e.g. yourname-20) to tag links properly (Operating Agreement §1 “Special Links”).
  3. Once supplied, the pipeline fills product fields only from each product's official page, activates the queued content library CTAs, and unlocks pricing/review/commission analysis.

Until then, this site never sends visitors to the broken links — they are quarantined in the Link Manager with the evidence attached.
