The design and editorial contract

The design and content-production suggestion layer: a paste-ready design prompt, design suggestions with current implementation states, the article topic queue, organic-traffic tactics, editorial voice rules and a decision register. Every row is a suggestion for you to approve, reject or modify — the status column records which of them the repository already satisfies today.

How this page fits the other registers.

Suggestions is the governance queue (SG-01…): what to decide and what it costs. Questions is what is not known yet (Q-01…). Decisions & handover settles strategy — which niche, which channels, what to measure. Content and traffic plan is the proposal view of the article queue — intent, angle and citations per topic. This page is the design and content-production layer: how the site should look and read, whether each proposed article has shipped yet, and the tactics that turn the strategy into published pages. Product facts are not here — they live in the full dataset, each with its official source.

The design prompt

Paste the block below into any designer or AI assistant used to restyle the site. It describes the site exactly as it is today, names the components and rules that must survive a redesign, and ends with the acceptance criteria every change has to pass. Nothing in it asks for a claim the site cannot source.

ZONE1 KITCHEN — CANONICAL PUBLICATION BRIEF

PURPOSE
Help a reader compare capacity, counter space and care. Products and buying decisions first; evidence available without making the page look like an audit dashboard. The configured publication is https://kanlerxz87-cyber.github.io/Zone1/.

LAYOUT AND BRAND
Use the warm-paper / forest-green design tokens in publication/theme.css, self-hosted DM Serif Display headings and DM Sans body/UI. Use a masthead, four simple reader links, then the exact Associate disclosure in a visible strip immediately BELOW the masthead on every page. Repeat it in the footer. One native Workspace menu holds all owner/evidence pages; the footer repeats direct access and a complete index. No sidebar, audit-stat hero, revenue scenario or sticky purchase overlay on reader pages.

Lead with a short buying question, three concise format/product cards, a contextual comparison route, practical guides and one modest seasonal feature. Product pages explain who might compare the format, what is documented, and what must be checked. The comparison uses source-linked model-specific capacity, footprint, power and care, not a winner score.

EVIDENCE AND LINKS
Keep the five exact submitted URLs. Observed redirect/title/ASIN, intended listing/ASIN, confirmation date/source and enrolled-account tests are separate fields. shopnow047c-20 is selected but unvalidated and unapplied; never invent approval or add it before the required tests. Every actual outbound product destination is sponsored. Main CTAs say Amazon and repeat the intended-listing/attribution caveat. Every numeric table row has primary evidence; unknown factual values stay blank and visibly flagged.

There is no authorised Amazon price/rating integration. Say that, rather than claiming Amazon bans every permitted price display. Brand snapshots are dated and labelled. No current offer, discount, rating, test, serving yield, performance ranking or personal experience is inferred. Flag F96 records conflicting cord instructions inside the Emeril manual: do not select the permissive version.

AUTHORSHIP, IMAGERY AND VOICE
Byline the publication and disclose AI-assisted document research. Do not invent a human editor or an expert persona. Use original format diagrams labelled not to scale. The one conceptual AI kitchen scene is decorative, visibly labelled and not a photograph/test of a specific model. Actual product photography needs original work or documented rights.

Write in plain, question-led language. Be specific without stuffing paragraphs with table display labels. State the useful trade-off, its source and the consequence. Avoid sweeping superlatives, fabricated social proof, fake urgency, generic intros and filler. “Trendy” means an editorial hypothesis unless dated first-party evidence establishes demand.

CONTENT AND READER LOOP
Use the four existing intent clusters. Improve the canonical answer to a question before adding a competing article. The operating plan is weekly source review and at most one useful new/substantially revised guide per fortnight, published only after claims/source/link/disclosure/build checks. It is not an unattended scheduler. RSS is the working reader-subscription mechanism; no fake email form.

SEARCH AND MEASUREMENT
Keep one canonical reader sitemap, regenerated feed, appropriate index/noindex metadata and truthful JSON-LD. Source-read dates are not publication timestamps. The optional Search Console token stays blank without real property verification. No traffic/ranking promise.

The five measurement events are defined, with only four emitted by a browser. The first-party aggregate collector is implemented/tested, not deployed; the ledger is empty. Keep collection disabled until controlled same-origin hosting, infrastructure-log minimisation and privacy approval exist. Orders come only from authorised reports. No projection, conversion score or placement-improvement claim without dated eligible first-party data.

ACCEPTANCE
Run build.py, verify.py, root/platform tests, annex reproducibility and link/source gates. Audit real rendered pages on narrow/desktop viewports and without JavaScript; check disclosure order/visibility, navigation, keyboard focus, overflow, local assets and outbound link qualification. Keep historical generators read-only. Open this branch’s PR, wait for checks, merge by explicit PR number and confirm the actual deployed Pages content before logging completion.

Editorial voice rules

The voice half of the anti-slop rules. The enforcement half — the banned openers, the arithmetic guard, the claim checks — lives on decisions.html and in build.py, where the build fails if a rule is broken.

Current editorial voice rules
#RuleWhyEvidence
V-1One topic per page, and every page stamps when its sources were readFocus for the reader, freshness signal for search engines, and an honest record of what the page is based on.S8
V-2Human voice: contractions, questions, specific numbers instead of 'very/really/incredible', short and long sentences mixedThe single fastest way to stop reading like generated filler. Specifics are also the only thing this site has - every number is sourced.voice rules, session-3b99448e ↗
V-3Label vendor claims: anything the manufacturer says about its own product is marked a brand claim, not a tested factA repeated brand claim presented as an finding is the classic fake-review pattern; labelling it is both honest and compliant.S7 S8
V-4Working titles are questions or decisions a reader types - never announcements like 'The Ultimate Guide to...'Announcement titles are the loudest AI-slop tell, and they match no search query anyone types.S30
V-5Headlines name the thing and the payoff; no curiosity-gap clickbaitA reader arriving from search must know in one second what the page does for them. Curiosity gaps earn clicks and lose trust, and trust is this site's only product.build.py voice guards ↗
V-6Numbers in headlines only when the page sources them in the bodyA headline number is a promise the body has to keep; an unsourced one is the slop tell readers notice first.build.py voice guards ↗
V-7Seasonal pages carry their year and their expiry, and never a countdownSeasonal content must age honestly. Countdown timers are urgency theatre and contradict the site's deliberate no-Amazon-prices rule (Program Policies 2(b)).S2
V-8A product page may say what a machine is for and what to check before buying. It may not say what it is like to live with.The first is a reading of published documents; the second is an experience nobody here has had, and FTC guidance makes having used the product the test for describing it.S8
V-9A published brand price is a dated brand snapshot, never an Amazon/current-offer claim. Amazon content display requires the applicable authorised route, not a blanket policy slogan.Silence about price reads as an omission; an unlabelled price reads as today's price. Naming both the figure and the policy that keeps the other one off the page is the version a reader can check.S2
