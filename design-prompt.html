Design prompt pack: review before anything changes

The design and content-production suggestion layer: a paste-ready design prompt, design suggestions with live status, the article topic queue, organic-traffic tactics, editorial voice rules and a decision register. Every row is a suggestion for you to approve, reject or modify — the status column records which of them the repository already satisfies today.

How this page fits the other registers.

Suggestions is the governance queue (SG-01…): what to decide and what it costs. Questions is what is not known yet (Q-01…). Decisions & handover settles strategy — which niche, which channels, what to measure. Content and traffic plan is the proposal view of the article queue — intent, angle and citations per topic. This page is the design and content-production layer: how the site should look and read, whether each proposed article has shipped yet, and the tactics that turn the strategy into published pages. Product facts are not here — they live in the full dataset, each with its official source.

The design prompt

Paste the block below into any designer or AI assistant used to restyle the site. It describes the site exactly as it is today, names the components and rules that must survive a redesign, and ends with the acceptance criteria every change has to pass. Nothing in it asks for a claim the site cannot source.

ZONE1 KITCHEN — CURRENT DESIGN & EDITORIAL BRIEF (19 November 2026)

PURPOSE
A useful, polished kitchen buying publication, openly AI-assisted and documentation-based. Never disguise it as a human test lab. Three documented air-fryer formats are compared; five submitted links are recorded separately from the account holder's unconfirmed product intent. No conversion, ranking, profit or popularity result exists.

INFORMATION ARCHITECTURE
Reader navigation: Air fryers / Compare / The journal / Our approach / RSS. One Workspace group exposes every owner dashboard; repeat the complete index in the footer. The front page leads with the buyer's job and format choices, practical existing guides, a seasonal edit and a quiet method note. No dashboard metric wall or sidebar on the reader first screen.

VISUAL SYSTEM
Maintain publication/style.css and publication/app.js; build.py emits canonical assets unchanged. Warm paper, forest ink/green, terracotta and restrained serif headings with system-sans body/UI. Keep readable body copy, visible keyboard focus, responsive native details menus, independently scrollable source tables and reduced-motion support. Use 44px as the control-design preference, not a claim of WCAG conformance. WCAG 2.5.8 AA is 24px with exceptions/spacing; 2.5.5 AAA is 44px. Compact source chips have 24px targets. Test, do not assert accessibility from CSS alone.

DISCLOSURE & IMAGERY
The exact sentence 'As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases.' sits in a visible strip BELOW the masthead and BEFORE main content, with a footer repetition. Keep material-connection information near the action. Product purchase buttons remain gated on documented intended listings and enrolled-Associates destination/report tests. Every Amazon product anchor, including citations, has rel=sponsored.
Generic AI-generated kitchen atmosphere is permitted only when labeled editorial illustration, not a product photograph or test scene. Format drawings are explicitly not to scale. No Amazon imagery is copied. Rights-cleared model photography is a separate unresolved route; never generate realistic product evidence or invent an owner licence.

EVIDENCE & VOICE
Every factual table row has official evidence; absent values are blank and flagged. A registered-host link proves traceability, not that a claim is true. Read the relevant passage. Attribute brand claims, dates and configuration. Retain conflicting source statements and publish corrections; never pick the more convenient passage of a safety manual. Do not invent product experience, an editor, credentials, customer testimonials or awards. Titles and target queries are editorial hypotheses until authorized reports supply demand evidence. Human-sounding means precise and helpful, not falsely human-authored.

MONEY & MEASUREMENT
Preserve all five exact supplied links. Intended ASIN/listing/date/source and observed redirects are separate. shopnow047c-20 is selected, not confirmed active or applied. A missing tag on a landing URL proves nothing about attribution. Do not manufacture replacement links from candidate ASINs.
No Amazon price, rating, availability or review data is displayed. §2(b) has specified Amazon-served/API price routes; this site has no authorized feed. No price alerts, scraping, fake clicks, revenue forecasts or training on restricted Program Content.
The single v2 contract is page_view, outbound_click, article_engagement (60 active seconds, not completion), comparison_interaction (product-guide activation, not table visibility), and operator-only affiliate_report_import. Production collection is off; local fixture tests are not hosting or attribution proof. No unique-session or reported-order figure is inferred from browser events. The campaign ledger remains empty without authorized reports.

SEARCH & CADENCE
Keep reader canonicals, truthful WebSite/Article/BreadcrumbList metadata, sitemap and RSS generated together. Owner/research pages are noindex,follow. scripts/stage_pages.py wraps historical deployment copies as noindex archives without rewriting their sources. No robots disallow that prevents crawlers reading noindex. Do not advance all source/article dates merely by rebuilding; record substantive corrections individually.
Review one buyer question each Tuesday from 24 November 2026, at most one source-approved guide. New writing is paused pending production measurement/access. Monthly query-led review needs actual Search Console/property access and authorized reports. No invented verification token, indexing promise, trend score or high-conversion label.

DECISIONS & ACCEPTANCE
Keep suggestions, questions and the decision brief reviewable. Actions under the standing autonomous/reversible policy go into decision-log.implemented, never owner choices[]. Account/privacy/rights/intent permissions cannot be inferred from silence.
Before merging: run scripts/ci-check.sh, the canonical pre-build hash check, Python/Node contract tests, staged-artifact archive checks and local HTTPS real-browser checks. Confirm keyboard/no-JS/print and narrow layouts. Then verify Actions and live Pages read-back. A passed build is not proof of legal compliance, semantic source adequacy, traffic or sales.

Editorial voice rules

The voice half of the anti-slop rules. The enforcement half — the banned openers, the arithmetic guard, the claim checks — lives on decisions.html and in build.py, where the build fails if a rule is broken.

Editorial voice rules
#RuleWhyEvidence
V-1One topic per page, and every page stamps when its sources were readFocus for the reader, freshness signal for search engines, and an honest record of what the page is based on.S8
V-2Human voice: contractions, questions, specific numbers instead of 'very/really/incredible', short and long sentences mixedThe single fastest way to stop reading like generated filler. Specifics are also the only thing this site has - every number is sourced.voice rules, session-3b99448e ↗
V-3Label vendor claims: anything the manufacturer says about its own product is marked a brand claim, not a tested factA repeated brand claim presented as an finding is the classic fake-review pattern; labelling it is both honest and compliant.S7 S8
V-4Working titles are questions or decisions a reader types - never announcements like 'The Ultimate Guide to...'Announcement titles are the loudest AI-slop tell, and they match no search query anyone types.S30
V-5Headlines name the thing and the payoff; no curiosity-gap clickbaitA reader arriving from search must know in one second what the page does for them. Curiosity gaps earn clicks and lose trust, and trust is this site's only product.build.py voice guards ↗
V-6Numbers in headlines only when the page sources them in the bodyA headline number is a promise the body has to keep; an unsourced one is the slop tell readers notice first.build.py voice guards ↗
V-7Seasonal pages carry their year and their expiry, and never a countdownSeasonal content must age honestly. Countdown timers are urgency theatre and contradict the site's deliberate no-Amazon-prices rule (Program Policies 2(b)).S2
V-8A product page may say what a machine is for and what to check before buying. It may not say what it is like to live with.The first is a reading of published documents; the second is an experience nobody here has had, and FTC guidance makes having used the product the test for describing it.S8
V-9A price on this site is a brand's own list price, labelled as such, with the reason Amazon's figure is absent in the same breath.Silence about price reads as an omission; an unlabelled price reads as today's price. Naming both the figure and the policy that keeps the other one off the page is the version a reader can check.S2
