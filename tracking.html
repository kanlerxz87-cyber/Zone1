Click, Conversion Tracking & Optimization

The tracking stage measures link performance and optimizes campaigns continuously. Metrics are defined against Amazon's official Session and Qualifying Purchase rules.

Tracking workflow

  • 1 · Tag — publish correctly formatted tagged Special Links (mis-formatted links are disqualified).
  • 2 · Capture — record clicks and attribute them to a Session.
  • 3 · Convert — count Qualifying Purchases per the official Session / 89-day cart / 180-day ship rules.
  • 4 · Optimize — adjust placements, content and niches based on conversion data.

Optimization

The platform optimizes by ranking products on verified conversion and commission signals, rotating link placements, and expanding content around high-performing niches. Optimization only uses data that has passed the verification gate.
