What needs to match before a recommendation?

  1. The intended listing. Confirm which official Amazon listing the account holder means to promote; a redirect alone is not an answer.
  2. The exact variant. Match the manufacturer model number, probe configuration and region to that listing. A family page may describe several variants.
  3. The claim and its source. Read the exact model’s manual or certification documentation before repeating accuracy, response-time or heat-rating claims. No claim is filled from a similar product.
  4. The paid link. Test the enrolled Special Link and its report in Associates Central. A visible or missing tag= string is not the test. S41 S42
