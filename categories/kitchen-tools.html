Why this is a shelf and not a catalogue

A page whose cells are mostly blank is thin content, and thin affiliation is the failure mode Google's spam policies name (S30). So this pass publishes one comparison page for the cluster rather than two near-empty product pages, and the rows that no official document supports stay in the blank-and-flagged table on the Flagged page instead of as empty product pages.

Not merged into data/products.json and not published in compare.html or the capacity figures. The air-fryer comparison stays apples-to-apples: three appliances, the same spec rows, the same manuals. This cluster gets its own page and its own rows, because the useful comparison here is between two thermometers, and between a thermometer and the appliance it goes with.

No commission rate is published for either item. The Standard Commission Income Statement (S3) lists a 4.50% row for "Physical Books, Kitchen, Automotive" and a 3.00% row that includes "Home"; both products sit on Amazon pages under Home & Kitchen › Kitchen & Dining, and the rate that actually applies is the one the reporting system assigns to the ASIN's category, which no public document in this register states. The mapping is therefore left open rather than inferred.

Recorded rather than estimated: S3 — and see Q-30 for the one account-side read that would fill it.
