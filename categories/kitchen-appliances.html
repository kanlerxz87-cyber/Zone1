Why the category matters commercially

Amazon's published rate table (reproduced in full on Compliance) puts Kitchen at 4.50%, Home at 3.00% and the fallback row, All Other Categories, at 4.00% S3. A countertop cooking appliance could plausibly fall in any of those, and Amazon does not publish the assignment. That 1.5-point spread between the best and worst case is why the platform blueprint scores products on scenarios rather than a single number.
