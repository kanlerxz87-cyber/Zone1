Before choosing a thermometer

Check the exact model and probe configuration, its instructions for use and cleaning, and whether any app or hub is required. The CHEF iQ brand page has selectable probe configurations; the ThermoMaven catalogue includes different instant-read and wireless lines. Do not copy a specification from one variant to another.
