Make room for the right air fryer.

Before comparing presets, answer four questions: what you cook, how you divide it, where the machine will live, and how you’ll clean it.

By Zone1 Kitchen · AI-assisted document research · no hands-on testing · Sources checked 2026-11-19

Choose the layout before the model.

The Chefman dual-basket manual describes two 3-quart baskets with separate settings and Sync Finish. The 8-quart model uses a single basket. The Emeril is a 26-quart oven-style cavity with a documented rotisserie. Those layouts—not a universal capacity-to-people rule—are the useful starting point. S18 S12 S15

Give the counter a proper check.

The published size does not include the space you need to open the appliance and operate it as instructed. The 8-quart Chefman manual calls for at least 6 inches above and behind it. The Emeril manual calls for at least 5 inches above, behind and at the sides, and says not to operate under hanging cabinets. Check the manual for your exact model rather than applying either rule to another unit. S13 S15

Read the care rules before the feature list.

The two Chefman manuals permit their removable baskets and racks on the top rack of a dishwasher. That does not include the main unit. The Emeril manual recommends hand-washing accessories and says not to soak or dishwash the appliance. There is no basis here for calling all three equally easy to clean. S18 S13 S15

Check the claims that need a second look.

The 8-quart listing title advertises a 450 °F Hi-Fry option, while Chefman’s page for RJ38-SQSS-8T documents a 200–400 °F range. The Emeril brand page uses 25 presets in an icon and 24 in its description. Do not turn these into extra features by combining the larger claims. S12 S29 S14
