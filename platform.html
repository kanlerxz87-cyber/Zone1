Dashboard / Platform blueprint

An automated affiliate engine that survives an audit

The brief asks for automation, scale, data-driven optimisation and Amazon policy compliance. Those four pull in different directions, and the design below resolves the tension in one place: automate everything except the claims. Machines move data, check links, compute and report. A human signs off on what gets asserted about a product.

Design principles

Principle 1

Automate the repetitive, never the judgement. Link health checks, page builds, click/conversion ingestion, report generation and rate-table diffs are automated. Category assignment, product claims and publication decisions stay human-approved because a wrong automated claim is a policy breach (S2 §2(b)).

Principle 2

Program Content is display-only. Creators/PA API data may not be aggregated, analysed or repurposed, and may not train or fine-tune any model (S2 IP License §2(e)). So the analytics layer reads the operator's own first-party data only.

Principle 3

Never publish a number you cannot source. The site's whole trust position rests on this. Blank plus flagged beats plausible plus wrong.

Principle 4

Disclosure is structure, not a paragraph. Disclosure is generated into the layout so no new page can ship without it (S1 §5, S5, S8).

Principle 5

Ship pages before you apply. Review happens after three qualifying sales inside 180 days, and the site must already carry ~10 recent original posts (S4). Content first, application second.

Commission is computed on Qualifying Revenue, which Amazon defines as what it actually receives less shipping, gift wrap, handling, taxes, service charges, credits, rebates, card fees and bad debt — so a real payout is lower than the figure above S2. Conversion rate is the single biggest unknown and is yours to measure.

Reading the numbers honestly

At the Kitchen rate of 4.50% S3, a $199.99 list-priced unit yields about $9.00 of commission before Amazon's deductions for returns, shipping, tax and bad debt. That is the ceiling, not the expectation. The variables that actually decide the outcome — conversion rate, return rate, mix of products in the cart — are unknowable from any official source and must be measured. Any projection that shows more precision than that is selling something.

Amazon requires at least three qualifying sales within the first 180 days and states that personal orders do not qualify S4. Applying before the site has traffic and content is the most common way this fails, so the sequence above puts publication first.

Explicitly out of scope

These are the features a generic "AI affiliate platform" pitch would include. They are excluded here because each one breaches a specific rule:

  • Price history charts or price-drop alerts (S2 §3(y)).
  • Republishing Amazon customer reviews or star ratings (S2 §3(t)).
  • Scraping Amazon and feeding the result into a model (S2 IP License §2(e)).
  • Auto-generating product claims from any source other than the manufacturer.
  • Paid search on Amazon or Kindle marks (S2 §2(c)).
  • Incentivised clicks, rewards or giveaways tied to links (S2 §3(g)).
  • Any claim that the author tested or owns these appliances.

What this build deliberately does not do

No scraped Amazon dataset

A product-research engine built on Amazon page scraping would violate the IP License's restriction on aggregation and extraction S2. The research layer here works on manufacturer documentation and the operator's own performance data.

No autopilot publishing

Content is drafted automatically and published by a human. The failure mode of full autopublish is a confident, well-formatted, policy-breaking claim — which is precisely what this project exists to prevent.

Cookies & privacy

This site sets no cookies itself, runs no analytics, and has no comment forms or accounts. Following an Amazon link takes you to Amazon, whose own privacy notice and cookie practices apply from that point. No data you enter here is collected, because there is nowhere to enter it.

Amazon's Program Policies §3(e) require an accurate statement of cookie and third-party technology use on Associate sites. This notice satisfies that requirement for the current build; it must be updated the moment any analytics, embeds or affiliate-network script is added.
