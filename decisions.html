Useful publication now. Evidence before expansion.

This is an operating plan, not a niche scorecard or an earnings forecast. The current site can help a reader compare documented formats without pretending that account, traffic or conversion evidence exists.

Three paths, with their prerequisites

Keep the existing focus

Maintain the air-fryer guides and correct documented gaps before adding volume. This is the reversible working scope, not a proven high-demand niche.

Needed next: exact product confirmation, an editorial owner and appropriate query evidence.

Cadence and review alternatives

Expand only after identity

The two observed thermometer records have a notes page, not eligible product recommendations. Confirm intended variants and the official evidence before commissioning product guides.

Expansion paths and questions

Measure only after deployment

The aggregate collector is prepared and locally tested. Keep it off until the production host, exact same-origin route, proxy logging and visitor notice have been reviewed. Clicks never become orders by inference.

Privacy, cookies & local-only decision storage

This site sets no cookies itself, runs no analytics, and has no public accounts, comment forms or email-signup form. The analytics client is implemented but is not loaded: no production collector is deployed. No production event counts have been measured by this site.

The owner-only decision picker stores choices in this browser’s local storage. It sends nothing automatically; choices leave the page only when explicitly copied or downloaded. Use its clear button to remove the saved choices.

Hosting is separate: GitHub states that Pages logs visitors’ IP addresses for security. Any future analytics host or proxy also needs its own logging and retention review. Following an Amazon link takes you to Amazon, where Amazon’s privacy notice and cookie practices apply.
