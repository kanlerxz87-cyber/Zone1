The operating choices

Publish the useful answer. Measure the real result.

A source-led buying publication is implemented. Profitability, popularity and conversion performance are not established by a well-designed page or a weighted score.

The scope

A source-led kitchen buying publication: make capacity, counter space and care requirements easier to compare without pretending to test products.

Three documented appliance guides and two unconfirmed thermometer link notes reflect the submitted set, not a claim to cover a whole market. Brand/listing/model conflicts remain on the public review register. S10 S12 S14 S36 S37

The editorial rhythm

Review source changes weekly; prepare at most one useful new or substantially refreshed guide every two weeks. Publish only after primary-source, disclosure, link and build checks pass.

Do not publish a second article answering the same question. Improve the existing canonical guide instead. Hold a draft if its claims require unverified identity, unauthorised API content or invented first-party results.

operating plan, not a scheduled autonomous publisher. Review gift and sale-checking guides ahead of the relevant retail season. The November 2026 selection is editorial context, not proof of search demand or a deal.

The reader loop

Answer practical kitchen buying questions, connect the existing intent clusters, and offer the real RSS feed. No social account, email service, paid campaign, popularity badge or lead total is fabricated. Search work is canonical metadata, a maintained sitemap and useful internal links—not a traffic guarantee. S43 S44 S45

The measurement boundary

The four allowed browser events are page_view, outbound_click, article_engagement and comparison_interaction. affiliate_report_import is operator-only. The tested first-party collector stays disabled until real hosting/privacy gates pass. The campaign ledger remains empty. S2 S38

No revenue scenario, forecast or conversion score is used to justify current design. Do not turn brand list prices, candidate rates or synthetic browser tests into actual earnings. Actual orders and commission facts come only from authorised dated first-party reports. S1 S42

What remains a plan

Automated source monitoring, licensed content integrations, production measurement and campaign optimisation require the access, permissions, runtime and data described on the platform blueprint. The static generator does not create those prerequisites.

Privacy, local drafts & hosting
