The parallel register: fourteen questions with their known facts, gaps and next steps

14 questions. For each one: what the repository already holds (with its official source), what is still missing, how to find the missing fact, and which decision it unblocks. Nothing in the missing column has been filled in with an estimate.

Read this first. Q-01 and Q-06 decide most of the other choices: which products the links really point to, and which Amazon store the site can promote. Answer those two before the product pages are extended.
