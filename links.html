2 · Canonical link builder

Builds a properly formatted tagged link: https://www.amazon.com/dp/<ASIN>?tag=<TAG> — the format the Operating Agreement §1 requires for “Special Links”. Nothing is invented: invalid input is rejected.

ASIN rule: exactly 10 letters/digits (the three supplied IDs are 9 and will be rejected). Tag rule: your Associates tracking ID (usually ending -20 for the US program). No tag was supplied for this project (flag F03).
