Product link register: five supplied links, two kinds of statement

This page keeps two things apart that are easy to collapse into one. Intent is what a link was created to point at — only the account holder can state it, and only Associates Central can confirm it. Observation is what the link did when this repository followed it. A destination that reproduces every time is still not an intention.

Why they are not merged. The 'intent' block and the 'observation' block are different kinds of statement and are NEVER merged. 'intent' records what the account holder says a link was created to point at; it can only be filled by a human who has checked Associates Central. 'observation' records what the link actually did when this repository followed it. A destination that is reproducible is still not an intention. Copying an observed ASIN into the intent block is the exact error this register exists to prevent, and a build guard fails the build if it happens (see check_link_intent in build.py).

The procedure, in order.
  1. Sign in and confirm the supplied tracking ID appears under Manage your Tracking IDs. S36
  2. Generate one Special Link for one product, for that ASIN, with that ID.
  3. Open it. Confirm the destination is the product you intended.
  4. Confirm the click appears against that tracking ID in the tracking report.
  5. Only then repeat for the remaining products, and record each validated link above.

Steps 1–4 are the gate. Doing them for one link first proves the mechanism before five unconfirmed links are scaled across a growing article set. S2 See SG-38 and Q-28.

Cookies & privacy

This site sets no cookies itself, runs no analytics, and has no comment forms or accounts. The outbound-click counter on these pages counts clicks in the browser's own memory for the current visit and sends nothing anywhere, because no endpoint is configured yet. Following an Amazon link takes you to Amazon, whose own privacy notice and cookie practices apply from that point. No data you enter here is collected, because there is nowhere to enter it.

Amazon's Program Policies §3(e) require an accurate statement of cookie and third-party technology use on Associate sites. This notice satisfies that requirement for the current build; it updates automatically the moment an analytics endpoint is configured.
