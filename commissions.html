Rate mechanics (verified facts)

  • Commission formula: standard commission income = category rate × Qualifying Revenue.
  • Qualifying Revenue excludes shipping, taxes, gift wrap, fees and credits (Commission Income Statement s.1).
  • Link requirement: only purchases via properly tagged “Special Links” count (Operating Agreement §1) — the submitted links carry no tag (flag F03).
  • Redirecting Links are disqualified (Commission Income Statement s.2(e), per session d14c5409's compliance review S8) — relevant to short-link usage generally (flag F05).
  • Rate changes: Amazon updates the schedule over time; re-verify at the official page before making business decisions.
