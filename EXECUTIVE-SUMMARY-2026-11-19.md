# Zone1 Kitchen — executive brief

## Bottom line

Zone1 Kitchen now leads with a useful buying publication: three documented formats, readable
product guides, a question-led journal, comparison, buying checklist and RSS. Owner tools,
evidence and dashboards remain accessible through one Workspace group and the footer.

The implementation does **not** establish a functioning affiliate business. Intended listings,
Associates approval/attribution, a production analytics host and account-reported results are
still unconfirmed. The selected tracking ID **`shopnow047c-20`** is recorded, not applied. The
campaign ledger remains empty. No conversions, earnings, popularity or search demand are claimed.

## Delivered

- **Reader-first design:** a cream/forest visual system, self-hosted licensed fonts, responsive
  product shelf with working filters, keyboard-operable workspace menu and clear evidence routes.
  The required Associate statement sits under the masthead and repeats in the footer. All
  outbound product anchors, including product-listing citations, use `sponsored nofollow noopener`.
- **Honest editorial presentation:** transparent AI/document-based authorship; no hands-on test,
  expert identity or product photo invented. Generic food/preparation scenes are labelled AI
  illustrations, not a promoted appliance, tested recipe or cooking result.
- **Existing content corrected, not multiplied:** all 24 article bodies reviewed and rewritten in
  place. Their questions are retained; unsupported certainty is removed. Unknown original
  publication timestamps no longer inherit a source-read date. Actual edits and scoped document
  reads are separate metadata.
- **Measurement contract repaired:** four browser events and operator-only `affiliate_report_import`
  now share one tested schema. The Python collector stores daily aggregates, not raw reader IDs,
  full URLs, ASINs or tracking IDs. Incompatible PHP/Worker examples are retired. Both production
  switches remain off; the client is not loaded by the published pages.
- **Reviewable decisions:** each suggestion now has an explicit implementation state separate from
  its alternatives, recommendation and missing-information questions. The decision desk has real
  search and no invented numeric priority ranking. No owner choice was manufactured.

## Findings that matter

1. **A real safety-document conflict remains open.** The FAFO-001 manual says “NEVER use with an
   extension cord” in its safeguards but later describes longer-cord use. The article had quoted
   only one passage. It now records both and does not infer permission. Manufacturer clarification
   is still needed for the exact unit. [Official manual](https://cdn.shopify.com/s/files/1/0687/4388/8948/files/FAFO-001_VN_32451_EF_IB_T22-5004282-C_1.pdf)
2. **Several earlier absence claims were false.** The Chefman 8 Qt manual explicitly warns against
   aerosol spray. The Emeril manual contains a rotisserie-spit weight limit, preset settings and
   an internal-temperature chart. These errors survived automated citation checks: a source link
   does not prove the prose is accurate. [Chefman manual](https://cdn.shopify.com/s/files/1/0576/5755/7158/files/RJ38-SQSS-8T-UG-US.pdf)
   · [Emeril manual](https://cdn.shopify.com/s/files/1/0687/4388/8948/files/FAFO-001_VN_32451_EF_IB_T22-5004282-C_1.pdf)
3. **The old analytics pieces disagreed.** Browser event names/payloads, the Python collector and
   endpoint examples were not one functioning protocol. Cross-language and local HTTP tests now
   exercise the repaired contract. That is not evidence of production deployment or an Amazon order.
4. **Historical metadata was being misused.** Article/RSS publication fields were generated from
   the collection’s November 14 source-read date, with an invented 09:00 time. Unknown publication
   fields are now omitted rather than guessed; feed GUIDs stay stable.
5. **Some gates were weaker than their descriptions.** Rendered-source tests had missed source
   directories with no index page and did not establish semantic accuracy. Strict `/Zone1/`
   routing and visible citation-array checks now supplement—not replace—editorial review.
