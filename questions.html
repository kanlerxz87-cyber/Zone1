The questions in full

Product identity

Q-01 · Product identity

Which exact manufacturer model number does each of the three supplied Amazon links point to?

Missing - discoverable

Why it matters. Three separate Chefman 6-quart dual-basket models exist with different documented temperature ranges and functions. If the wrong model is on a product page, every specification below it is a misstatement about a product the site is paid to recommend.

What we know, and can prove

  • All three link.amazon short links resolve to the same three ASINs on every recorded fetch: B0CDY277D3, B09B7SB46R and B08DKYBTPH (7 observation rounds, 2026-11-13 to 2026-11-15).
  • The three destination pages carry the listing titles recorded in data/products.json, except the Emeril title, which changed between 2026-11-14 and 2026-11-15 (flag F38).
  • The bare 9-character codes in the short links (B04G1MEwY, B02NbDxp3, B00hJ57DV) are not ASINs and return 'Page Not Found' when used as /dp/ identifiers. Re-tested 2026-11-15.

What is missing

The 'Model Number' row inside each listing's Product information table. Amazon publishes it on the page but not through any open interface, so it can only be read in a browser.

How to find it out

Open each of the three links, scroll to Product information, read the Model Number row. Five minutes total. Record all three strings.

Official sources. Chefman 6 Qt dual-window model page (RJ38-SQPF-3TDB-2W) ↗ Chefman 8 Qt model page ↗ Flag register rows F2, F3, F32, F33 ↗

Q-02 · Product identity

Is the Chefman 8 Qt listing the RJ38-SQSS-8T the site maps it to, or the RJ38-8T-BLACK that documents the 450 F Hi-Fry?

Missing - discoverable

Why it matters. The listing title advertises '450 F Hi-Fry ... Bake, Dehydrate, Frozen'. The RJ38-SQSS-8T brand page and manual document 200-400 F and four food presets, with no Hi-Fry. One of the two is simply a different machine. This is the sharpest unresolved conflict in the dataset.

What we know, and can prove

  • The Amazon listing title contains '450 F Hi-Fry Option' and 'Bake, Dehydrate, Frozen'.
  • The chefman.com page for RJ38-SQSS-8T states a maximum temperature of 400 F.
  • Chefman publishes a separate RJ38-8T-BLACK that documents 450 F Hi-Fry and exactly those four functions (flag F33).

What is missing

The model number printed on the listing itself, which settles the question in one line.

How to find it out

Same act as Q-01. Read the Model Number row on the B08DKYBTPH listing.

Official sources. Chefman 8 Qt page - 400 F maximum ↗ RJ38-SQSS-8T owner's manual ↗

Attribution

Q-04 · Attribution

Which Associates tag is inside the three supplied short links, and does it belong to this account?

Needs account access

Why it matters. This is the only question on the page whose answer determines whether the site earns any money at all. Program Policies section 2(a) requires Special Links to use the Associates ID assigned to you; section 2(b) requires the tag to appear as a parameter in each link.

What we know, and can prove

  • The links are link.amazon short links, so the redirect target is observable but the embedded tag is not.
  • The redirect target carries Amazon's own parameters (lv=shuf, btn_ref=srctok-..., plpRedirect=mhFallback) but no tag= parameter is visible in the landing URL.

What is missing

The full tagged URL. It is inside the short link and is only visible from the account that created it, or by reading the click report.

How to find it out

Option 1: open each link while signed in to Associates Central and confirm the click appears against your tracking ID. Option 2: rebuild each link as a full amazon.com URL with your tag and use that instead.

Official sources. Program Policies, section 2(a) and 2(b) ↗ Tracking IDs and sub-tags ↗

Q-05 · Data access

Do you have, or can you get, Creators API access?

Needs account access

Why it matters. Program Policies section 2(b) permits displaying Amazon price and availability only when Amazon serves the link or when the data comes from Creators API or PA API. Section 6(t) restricts customer reviews and star ratings to API-served content. Without it, the comparison table cannot carry a price or a rating, by law rather than by choice.

What we know, and can prove

  • The rate table and all program documents are readable without signing in.
  • The site currently displays no Amazon price, rating or review count on any page, and that is correct rather than an omission.

What is missing

Whether the account qualifies for Creators API, and whether any credentials exist.

How to find it out

Associates Central > Tools > Creators API (or PA API). Access requires qualifying sales. Record approved / not approved.

Catalogue data

Q-06 · Catalogue data

What is the product weight of the Emeril 26 QT French Door, and is the weight published for the two Chefman models the product weight or the packaged weight?

The source does not state it

Why it matters. Counter space and lifting weight are two of the few things a buyer can decide from a page without touching the product, which makes them unusually load-bearing for a 26-quart oven. Separately, the two Chefman weight figures this site publishes are contradicted by the same brand's own commerce endpoint, so the number may be right and the label on it wrong.

What we know, and can prove

  • Exterior dimensions are published for all three products from official sources: the Chefman 6 Qt from its product page (S10), the Chefman 8 Qt from its product page (S12), and the Emeril 26 QT from its product page (S14).
  • Warranty terms are published for all three: 1-year Chefman assurance (S10), limited 1-year from the owner's manual (S13), and 1-year warranty (S14).
  • Product weight is published for both Chefman models from their specification blocks: 13.3 lb / 6.03 kg for the 6 Qt (S10) and 12.8 lb / 5.8 kg for the 8 Qt (S12).
  • The same brand's own commerce endpoint, read on 2026-11-15, reports 14.51 lb / 6,582 g for RJ38-SQPF-3TDB-2W (S32) and 13.4 lb / 6,078 g for RJ38-SQSS-8T (S33) - both higher, and neither page says which figure is the product and which is the package (flag F51).
  • NARROWED 2026-11-15 (arena/63a2c638, flag F52): this row previously asked for dimensions, weight and warranty for all three products and was filed as 'not_stated'. Eight of those nine cells were already published from official sources in data/products.json. Only one cell is genuinely open.

What is missing

Two things, and only two. (1) The Emeril 26 QT product weight - neither the brand product page (S14) nor the owner's manual (S15) states one. (2) A statement from Chefman, on either page, saying whether its published weight is the product or the packaged weight.

How to find it out

For the Emeril: read the 'Product information' or specification table on the brand page and the specifications page of the owner's manual; if neither states a weight, leave the cell blank and say so rather than converting a shipping weight. For the Chefman labels: quote the sentence next to the weight in each Specifications block, and quote the endpoint field, then decide which one the site publishes - or publish both with their labels.

Official sources. Chefman 6 Qt page ↗ Chefman 8 Qt page - Specifications block ↗ Emeril French Door 360 page ↗

Authorship

Q-09 · Authorship

Who is the named author, and what may the site honestly claim about how content is produced?

Needs account access

Why it matters. 16 CFR 465.6 makes it deceptive for a business to misrepresent that a site it controls provides independent reviews. The site currently describes itself as 'an independent affiliate publisher' while every product page carries affiliate links, and it does not disclose that analysis is documentation-based and machine-assisted.

What we know, and can prove

  • No page on the site names an author or describes the production method.
  • The footer states no hands-on testing is claimed anywhere on the site.
  • 16 CFR 255.5 requires disclosure of material connections that a significant minority of the audience would not expect.

What is missing

The name to publish, whether an editorial human reviews each page before publication, and whether AI assistance will be disclosed.

How to find it out

A decision. If nothing else, publishing the method is enough: 'every number is quoted from a manufacturer document or the CFR, drafted with AI assistance and checked against the source'. That sentence is true today and is not currently on the site.

Official sources. 16 CFR 465.6 ↗ 16 CFR 255.5 ↗ FTC Endorsement Guides Q&A ↗

Economics

Q-11 · Economics

Which category has Amazon actually assigned to each ASIN, and therefore which commission rate applies?

Missing - discoverable

Why it matters. Kitchen pays 4.50% and the fallback row pays 4.00%. A 0.50-point difference moves the break-even traffic estimate by about 12 percent, which decides whether this niche is worth the content investment.

What we know, and can prove

  • The published Table 1 lists Physical Books, Kitchen and Automotive at 4.50%, and All Other Categories at 4.00%.
  • Amazon does not publish an ASIN-to-category mapping, which is why the site shows both candidate rows.
  • All three listings display the same breadcrumb: Home & Kitchen > Kitchen & Dining > Small Appliances > Fryers > Air Fryers.
  • NEW 2026-11-15 (arena/63a2c638): the two Chefman listings each render an Amazon browse-node breadcrumb reading 'Home & Kitchen > Kitchen & Dining > Small Appliances > Fryers > Air Fryers' (S16 for B0CDY277D3, S29 for B08DKYBTPH). That breadcrumb is now published as a sourced fact on each product page.
  • The same breadcrumb does NOT settle this question. The Commission Income Statement's categories (S3) are commission categories defined by the Associates programme; browse nodes are a shopper-navigation taxonomy; Amazon publishes no mapping between them. The breadcrumb begins 'Home & Kitchen' while S3 carries both 'Kitchen' at 4.50% and 'Home' at 3.00%, so it cannot even indicate which of the two it resembles. See flag F53.
  • The Emeril browse node is unknown: three fetches of ASIN B09B7SB46R on 2026-11-15 were answered with Amazon's automated 'Continue shopping' checkpoint rather than the listing page, so no breadcrumb was ever rendered and none was recorded (S28).

What is missing

Confirmation of the category Amazon applies at commission time. The breadcrumb is strong evidence; the Earnings report is proof.

How to find it out

The rate is not discoverable from any public page - it is only observable after the fact, in the Associates reports, where each qualifying purchase shows the rate actually applied. Read the category column on the first few real orders and the question answers itself. Publishing a rate before then means publishing a guess about how Amazon classified the product.

Official sources. Standard Commission Income Statement, Table 1 ↗ Chefman 6 Qt listing - visible breadcrumb ↗

Q-12 · Repository integrity

Is there any published statement on this site that a live official source contradicts today?

Missing - discoverable

Why it matters. A site whose entire premise is that every value is sourced cannot carry a stale value next to a source link that no longer says it. That is worse than an unsourced value, because the citation lends it false authority.

What we know, and can prove

  • This pass fetched all three supplied links, the Commission Income Statement, the Program Policies page, 16 CFR 255.5, 16 CFR Part 465 and both manufacturers' product pages on 2026-11-15.
  • One published value was found stale and is corrected in this pass: the Emeril listing title, which now reads '24 One-Touch Preset Options' where the stored value read '24 cooking functions' (flag F38).
  • One citation defect was found and corrected: the source record S16 is registered as the B0CDY277D3 URL but was cited for all three listing titles, so two of the three citations pointed at a different product's page (flag F39).
  • One register-integrity defect was found and corrected: two different rows were both numbered F36 (flag F40).

What is missing

A standing re-read schedule. Review counts and listing titles change without notice, and no page on the site currently records when a value was last read except the re-verification ledger.

How to find it out

Re-read every value on a fixed cadence and publish the date next to each volatile figure. The re-verification ledger is the right home for this; it needs a schedule rather than a one-off pass.

Register accuracy

Q-15 · Register accuracy

Is there any statement published on this site that a live official source contradicts today?

Missing - discoverable

Why it matters. Every register on this site is only as good as its last check. Three separate defects in this project's history have been the same defect - a value that was true when it was written and is no longer, or a register row that describes the data as it used to be (F14 stale headline, F38 stale listing title, F52 a question row describing eight published values as missing). A page that claims to be verified needs a dated, repeatable answer to whether that claim still holds.

What we know, and can prove

  • Round 9 (2026-11-15, arena/63a2c638) re-read S2, S3, S10, S12, S14, S16 and S29 against every value they support and found no published value contradicted by its source.
  • The same round found two accuracy defects that were not value errors: F52 (a questions row overstating what is missing) and F51 (two official brand sources disagreeing on weight).
  • S28 could not be re-read on 2026-11-15 because Amazon served a bot checkpoint, so one row is carried forward unverified for this date rather than re-confirmed.

What is missing

A standing, dated check rather than a per-session one: which official sources were last re-read, on what date, and what changed. Today that exists as prose in each session's coordination record, not as a page a reader can check.

How to find it out

Re-read every source cited by a published value on a fixed schedule and record the date and the result per source. The reverification page already carries the per-value ledger; what is missing is the promise to run it again, and a visible date on that promise.
