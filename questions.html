Product identity

Q-01 · Product identity

Which exact manufacturer model number does each of the three supplied Amazon links point to?

Known and sourced

Why it matters. Three separate Chefman 6-quart dual-basket models exist with different documented temperature ranges and functions. If the wrong model is on a product page, every specification below it is a misstatement about a product the site is paid to recommend.

Answer. Answered for the ASIN-to-model step on 2026-11-16, without a human in a browser - which is the part that matters, because F49 had recorded that no automated read could reach this table and SG-01 had assigned it to the owner as a five-minute browser job. Each listing's own Product information block carries the code: B0CDY277D3 publishes Model Number and Part Number RJ38-SQPF-3TDB-2W; B08DKYBTPH publishes Part Number RJ38-SQSS-8T (its Model Number row holds the string 'Large Air Fryer'); B09B7SB46R publishes Model Number and Part Number 4-00675-02X-VN, which is the brand page's 4-00675-02A-VN with one character changed. The first two agree with the manufacturer documents the site already held, so those two rows are now sourced twice. The third does not, and is published as a conflict (F66) rather than resolved. What this does NOT answer is which product the three supplied link.amazon tokens were meant to point at: the tokens are 9 characters, are not ASINs, and the observed destinations arrive through a fallback redirect (F7, F10). That half still belongs to the account holder.

What we know, and can prove

  • All three link.amazon short links resolve to the same three ASINs on every recorded fetch: B0CDY277D3, B09B7SB46R and B08DKYBTPH (7 observation rounds, 2026-11-13 to 2026-11-15).
  • The three destination pages carry the listing titles recorded in data/products.json, except the Emeril title, which changed between 2026-11-14 and 2026-11-15 (flag F38).
  • The bare 9-character codes in the short links (B04G1MEwY, B02NbDxp3, B00hJ57DV) are not ASINs and return 'Page Not Found' when used as /dp/ identifiers. Re-tested 2026-11-15.

What is missing

Nothing for the model step. For the link attribution: a statement from the account holder, or the Associates Central link builder showing which ASIN each short link was created for. Neither is reachable from this repository.

How to find it out

Done 2026-11-16 by session ba29c641: the Product information block was read off all three listing pages and published on the product pages and in reports/asin-model-mapping-2026-11-16.md. The residual part - link-to-ASIN intent - can only be settled by whoever created the short links.

Official sources. Chefman 6 Qt dual-window model page (RJ38-SQPF-3TDB-2W) ↗ Chefman 8 Qt model page ↗ Flag register rows F2, F3, F32, F33 ↗

Q-02 · Product identity

Is the Chefman 8 Qt listing the RJ38-SQSS-8T the site maps it to, or the RJ38-8T-BLACK that documents the 450 F Hi-Fry?

Known and sourced

Why it matters. The listing title advertises '450 F Hi-Fry ... Bake, Dehydrate, Frozen'. The RJ38-SQSS-8T brand page and manual document 200-400 F and four food presets, with no Hi-Fry. One of the two is simply a different machine. This is the sharpest unresolved conflict in the dataset.

Answer. Read from the listing on 2026-11-16, and the answer is 'the listing says both'. Its Part Number row reads RJ38-SQSS-8T - the code the brand page and the manual publish for the 200-400 F stainless model - and its own Features & Specs table states a 400 F maximum, while its title advertises '450 F Hi-Fry Option' with Bake/Dehydrate/Frozen. Because Chefman separately publishes RJ38-8T-BLACK at 450 F Hi-Fry with exactly those four functions (S26, re-read live 2026-11-16), the two explanations F3 and F33 hold - a title carried over from another SKU, or a second model listed under the first one's part number - are both still open, and the listing's self-contradiction is now evidence in the case rather than a third site disagreeing. What the read does settle is the direction of the conflict, and what the site may not say: no copy here markets a 450 F capability for this product, and the temperature ceiling of the unit that ships is left unasserted.

What we know, and can prove

  • The Amazon listing title contains '450 F Hi-Fry Option' and 'Bake, Dehydrate, Frozen'.
  • The chefman.com page for RJ38-SQSS-8T states a maximum temperature of 400 F.
  • Chefman publishes a separate RJ38-8T-BLACK that documents 450 F Hi-Fry and exactly those four functions (flag F33).

What is missing

A statement from Chefman or the seller about which SKU sits behind ASIN B08DKYBTPH, or the model plate on a unit bought against it. The site will not pick between its own documents without one.

How to find it out

Done 2026-11-16 by session arena/ba29c641 (Part Number and Max Temperature rows read from the listing's Product information block; see reports/asin-model-mapping-2026-11-16.md). What a script cannot do: open the box.

Official sources. Chefman 8 Qt page - 400 F maximum ↗ RJ38-SQSS-8T owner's manual ↗

What is the product weight of the Emeril 26 QT French Door, and is the weight published for the two Chefman models the product weight or the packaged weight?

Answer. No answer is published here. The official sources listed below were read and do not settle it, or it cannot be settled from outside the account. The "How to find it out" line names the step that would.

What we know, and can prove

  • Exterior dimensions are published for all three products from official sources: the Chefman 6 Qt from its product page (S10), the Chefman 8 Qt from its product page (S12), and the Emeril 26 QT from its product page (S14).
  • Warranty terms are published for all three: 1-year Chefman assurance (S10), limited 1-year from the owner's manual (S13), and 1-year warranty (S14).
  • Product weight is published for both Chefman models from their specification blocks: 13.3 lb / 6.03 kg for the 6 Qt (S10) and 12.8 lb / 5.8 kg for the 8 Qt (S12).
  • The same brand's own commerce endpoint, read on 2026-11-15, reports 14.51 lb / 6,582 g for RJ38-SQPF-3TDB-2W (S32) and 13.4 lb / 6,078 g for RJ38-SQSS-8T (S33) - both higher, and neither page says which figure is the product and which is the package (flag F51).
  • NARROWED 2026-11-15 (arena/63a2c638, flag F52): this row previously asked for dimensions, weight and warranty for all three products and was filed as 'not_stated'. Eight of those nine cells were already published from official sources in data/products.json. Only one cell is genuinely open.
  • NEW EVIDENCE 2026-11-16 (arena/b23f12f0, round 11): the brand storefront's own machine-readable product record (S35) reports a variant weight of 27.04 lb / 12,265 g for SKU 4-00675-02A-VN. This is recorded as a candidate, not published as the Emeril's product weight, because the endpoint does not say whether its weight field is the product or the packaged weight - the same ambiguity flag F51 records for the two Chefman models, whose specification-block weights (13.3 lb and 12.8 lb) are both lower than their own endpoint weights (14.51 lb and 13.4 lb). Converting a shipping figure into a product specification is exactly the inference this project refuses to make.

What is missing

Two things, and only two. (1) The Emeril 26 QT product weight - neither the brand product page (S14) nor the owner's manual (S15) states one. (2) A statement from Chefman, on either page, saying whether its published weight is the product or the packaged weight.

How to find it out

For the Emeril: read the 'Product information' or specification table on the brand page and the specifications page of the owner's manual; if neither states a weight, leave the cell blank and say so rather than converting a shipping weight. For the Chefman labels: quote the sentence next to the weight in each Specifications block, and quote the endpoint field, then decide which one the site publishes - or publish both with their labels.
