Automated Product Research & Analysis

The research stage ingests the supplied ASINs and analyzes each across the dimensions that determine affiliate profitability. Findings are only recorded where an official source confirms the value.

Data gap: because the three listings could not be verified (Amazon blocks automated access), every analyzed value below is left blank and flagged. The research engine is implemented and ready to populate the moment corrected, verifiable links are supplied.

Research workflow

  • 1 · Ingest — extract ASINs from the supplied affiliate links.
  • 2 · Resolve — resolve each ASIN to an official Amazon listing (category, brand, price, rating, availability).
  • 3 · Trending & conversion — score trending momentum and conversion likelihood from verified listing signals.
  • 4 · Analyze — pricing, reviews, competition and commission potential.
  • 5 · Niche — identify profitable niches and rank candidates for content generation.
