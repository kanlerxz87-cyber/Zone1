The fine print is worth reading.

We make documentation-based kitchen buying guides. That means reading the published specifications and owner’s manuals, explaining their differences, and being clear about what those documents cannot tell you.

Who is writing?

Zone1 Kitchen is the organisational publishing name. This publication is AI-assisted: source research, drafting, editing and site code have been produced in agent sessions. There is no named human tester or independently verified professional credential behind these pages.

No appliance has been purchased, handled or cooked with for this publication. A byline names the publisher, not a fictional reviewer. This distinction follows the FTC’s warning against describing experiences an endorser has not had. S8

How do the links work?

As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases. Paid product links are labelled and marked rel="sponsored". No payment buys a measured winner here. S1 S31

The supplied links’ intended listings, enrolled-account status and attribution are not confirmed. A selected tracking ID is not proof of a functioning Special Link. The five-link record keeps intent and observed destinations separate. S41 S42

What earns a place in a guide?

  1. An identifiable source. Product facts cite a manufacturer page, exact model manual or a clearly dated official listing observation. A source beside a row is evidence of that row, not a fresh inspection of every page.
  2. A useful question. Articles explain a purchasing or ownership decision. We do not publish search-volume claims, “trending” labels or top-ten rankings without evidence. The topic plan is a proposal queue, not a demand forecast. S30
  3. A visible limitation. Conflicting documents stay in conflict; missing values stay blank and flagged. We cannot infer the performance, food quality, reliability or comfort of a product from its specifications.
  4. A dated change. Source-read dates are not silently reset by a build. New findings and corrections belong in the report and change record. The latest scoped review is 19 November 2026; it is not a claim that every historical source was rechecked.

Imagery, honestly described

The kitchen-preparation and food scenes on the front page are AI-generated editorial illustrations. They show no promoted appliance and are not evidence of a tested recipe, product result or real test kitchen. Each image has a visible caption and factual alternative text.

The small format drawings and data diagrams are original SVG illustrations, not commercial-product photographs and not to scale. We do not generate a likeness of a specific product or copy Amazon or manufacturer photography. Product photography remains blocked until original photographs or recorded permission exist; see SG-42 and Q-34.

DM Sans and DM Serif Display are served locally under the SIL Open Font License; the licence files are shipped with the fonts. DM Sans licence · DM Serif Display licence. No third-party font request is made.

Corrections and the publishing record

Found a mismatch? Use the repository’s issue tracker with the page, the disputed sentence and an official source. Do not post account details, reports containing customer data or credentials. Public issues are visible to others.

Privacy, cookies & local-only decision storage

This site sets no cookies itself, runs no analytics, and has no public accounts, comment forms or email-signup form. The analytics client is implemented but is not loaded: no production collector is deployed. No production event counts have been measured by this site.

The owner-only decision picker stores choices in this browser’s local storage. It sends nothing automatically; choices leave the page only when explicitly copied or downloaded. Use its clear button to remove the saved choices.

Hosting is separate: GitHub states that Pages logs visitors’ IP addresses for security. Any future analytics host or proxy also needs its own logging and retention review. Following an Amazon link takes you to Amazon, where Amazon’s privacy notice and cookie practices apply.
