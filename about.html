About Zone1 Kitchen

Useful buying guides. Clear limits.

This publication starts with the questions a kitchen purchase leaves you with: will it fit, what will it hold, and what will it ask of you after dinner?

Who writes this?

Zone1 Kitchen is the publication byline. The research, drafting and site build are AI-assisted. No named human editor has been established, so there is no invented expert biography, cook, test team or personal recommendation here. The source register makes the primary documents available for your own review.

Documents, not a test kitchen

No hands-on testing is claimed. No appliance was bought, handled or tested for these guides. Published dimensions, capacities and care rules come from the manufacturer or its manual. A label such as “worth comparing” is editorial guidance about those documents, not a claim about taste, noise, reliability or cooking speed. The FTC says you cannot talk about an experience with a product you have not tried. S8

When official documents disagree, the difference stays visible. An unconfirmed value stays blank; “not documented” is not the same as “not included.” See Flagged for review for the evidence and discovery steps.

How the links work

As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases. Eligible links may earn a commission. The selected ID is recorded, but account ownership and attribution have not been verified. The five submitted links have separate fields for their observed redirect and their intended listing; none has a completed enrolled-Associates test. Short-link uncertainty is not a finding that the link is invalid. S1 S5 S41

We do not display Amazon prices, availability, star ratings or review counts. This site has no authorised live Program Content integration. That is a limitation of this implementation—not a claim that Amazon prohibits every permitted price display. Read the link and content policy. S2

About the illustrations and imagery

The line drawings are original format diagrams, not photographs or exact depictions of a branded model. The front-page kitchen scene is AI-generated decorative imagery. It is not a real test kitchen, an appliance demonstration, proof of a cooking result or an image of the submitted products. No Amazon or manufacturer product photograph has been copied. Image provenance and the self-hosted fonts’ open licences are recorded in the publication asset notes.

Updates and corrections

Sources are dated on individual guides. A fresh layout does not refresh every product fact. The 19 November 2026 review report names exactly which official pages and sections were read, what changed, and what remains unverified. Reports from earlier passes remain available, not silently rewritten. The 20 November 2026 read covers the pages re-read for the product-page decision kit and the hosting-week guide, and lists the two brand-page contradictions that pass found without editing another session's register.

The operating plan is a weekly source review and at most one new or substantially refreshed guide every two weeks, subject to evidence and checks. This is not an already-running scheduler. The publishing plan distinguishes topics worth investigating from measured demand; no traffic, ranking or revenue outcome is promised.

Privacy and reader capture

This site sets no cookies itself, runs no analytics, and sends no measurement events. The self-hosted collector is implemented and tested, but is not deployed on this GitHub Pages site. Browser collection is disabled.

GitHub Pages separately logs visitor IP addresses for security. This publisher does not control those host logs. GitHub’s hosting notice. Following an Amazon link takes you to Amazon, where its own privacy and cookie practices apply. Workspace decision drafts, if used, stay in your browser’s local storage until cleared; they are not sent to a server.

RSS is the only reader-subscription mechanism currently offered. There is no working newsletter signup and no subscriber list.
