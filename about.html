Behind the guide

Good questions before
a purchase.

Zone1 Kitchen is a documentation-based kitchen buying publication. We help you compare formats, read specifications and spot the details worth checking before you buy.

Who publishes this?

Published as Zone1 Kitchen, maintained in the public kanlerxz87-cyber/Zone1 repository. Articles are AI-assisted and built from source-linked product records. A named human editor or expert reviewer has not been confirmed; none is invented here.

What did we actually test?

No hands-on product testing is claimed. We read official manufacturer pages and manuals, compare what they document, and link factual specifications to those sources. Cooking results, reliability, noise and real-life capacity are not established by this method.

“Documentation-based” is a limit, not a substitute for product experience. The FTC explicitly warns against describing an experience with a product you have not tried. S8

Our editorial rules

  1. Start with a real buying question.
  2. Put a primary source beside a factual claim.
  3. Keep unknown values blank.
  4. Show conflicts; do not average them away.
  5. No invented expert, test or testimonial.
  6. No ranking dressed up as a measurement.

How affiliate links work

As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases. We may earn a commission when a qualifying purchase is credited through an eligible link. Product-link controls and the site-wide disclosure are separate safeguards; a disclosure does not prove that a link tracks.

The selected tracking ID, shopnow047c-20, came from the project brief. Its account status and the intended product behind each submitted link remain unconfirmed. Purchase CTAs stay gated until the intended listing and an enrolled-Associates link/report test are recorded. We do not call the supplied short links invalid: their precise compliance and attribution status is unresolved. S1 S2 S41 S42

Amazon prices, availability, star ratings and customer-review content are not displayed. Links may take you to Amazon, where its own terms and privacy practices apply.

Where the illustrations come from

The front-page kitchen still life is AI-generated editorial art—not a photograph of a promoted product or proof of a cooking result. Format drawings are original generic illustrations, not scale drawings or manufacturer assets. Model-specific product photography has not been licensed.

How new work gets published

The project reviews one buyer question each Tuesday, starting 24 November 2026. This is a planning cadence, not an automatic promise to publish. New writing stays paused until production measurement/access is cleared; an approved guide still needs primary sources, disclosure review and link checks. Approved article records generate the journal, sitemap and RSS together.

Topics are hypotheses until dated query data supports them. Helpful content and accurate authorship matter more than volume; no search position or organic-traffic result is guaranteed. S44 S45

Corrections & contact

Found a model mismatch, source conflict or broken link? Open an issue in the public repository issue tracker, with the page URL and official source. Do not post account credentials, order information or personal data.

Unresolved findings appear on Flagged for Review. Product intent and observed redirects are kept in separate fields. Source dates describe when a document was read, not when an appliance was tested.

Privacy & site technologies +

This site sets no cookies itself, runs no analytics, and has no comment forms or accounts. This GitHub Pages build sends no analytics events: the proposed first-party collector is not deployed and browser collection remains disabled. Following an Amazon link takes you to Amazon, whose own privacy notice and cookie practices apply from that point. No data you enter on reader pages is collected. The owner-only decision picker stores drafts in your own browser, only when used; it does not submit them. GitHub Pages may retain connection logs for security under its own privacy policy.

Amazon's Program Policies §3(e) require an accurate statement of cookie and third-party technology use on Associate sites. This notice describes this build; it is not a legal-compliance determination and must be reviewed the moment any analytics, embeds or affiliate-network script is added. The outbound-click counter for this site is written and shipped in assets/analytics.js but is switched off, because no endpoint host has been chosen — no analytics request is sent. Hosting still entails connection metadata. The paragraph above is generated from that switch, so it cannot describe a behaviour the page does not have.
