How to read a status.
  • Delivered Implemented and visible on the published site.
  • Partial Implemented in part. The gap is named on the row.
  • Blocked Cannot be done from outside Amazon's own systems. No amount of work here closes it.
  • Not built Buildable today with the inputs already present, and not built yet.

Status is a claim about this repository, so each one is checkable: the 'where' paths must exist in the tree (the build fails if one does not), the evidence links open the file or the official page that settles it, and a row with no evidence must be a row whose honest answer is that nothing exists yet.

The three counts that matter, and why they are not zero.

Blocked rows are not unfinished work. Each one depends on a fact that exists only inside Amazon's own systems — the Associate tag, the approval status of the account, per-ASIN commission categories, customer reviews, conversion reports. No amount of work in this repository closes them, and the alternative to leaving them open is inventing a number, which is the one thing the brief forbids more plainly than any other.

Not built rows are the opposite: buildable today, not built yet. There are 1 of them, and traffic outcomes remain unproven — see the content and traffic plan for the channels Amazon's policies leave open.

What this register does not claim

A status here is a statement about this repository, not about the affiliate business. This site has three documented appliance guides, two unconfirmed thermometer notes, no verified campaign data and no confirmed active affiliate tag, so no row on this page should be read as evidence of revenue potential. The rows that would answer that — C-07, C-08, E-04 — are exactly the ones blocked.

Where a requirement could not be met, the row names the missing input and the clause that makes it missing rather than softening the status. Unverified values and unresolved conflicts are collected on Flagged for review, and the questions that a decision would have to answer first are on Questions and gaps.

Where this fits. This page is the coverage view. Suggestions is the queue of changes with options and a recommended path; Design prompt pack is the status view of what has already shipped; Questions and gaps is what must be answered before a decision. The s2 annex carries its own, earlier, 19-row register at s2/pages/requirements.html — it is not edited by this page, and the two disagreements between them are recorded as flags F59 and F60.
