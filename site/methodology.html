2. The three-state rule

Every field is in exactly one of three states, and the state is published alongside the value:

Verified

The value was read on an official page and the specific section is recorded. Two independent official pages agreeing strengthens it; a single official page is sufficient, and the source is always linked.

Flagged

Official sources disagree, or the only basis is a fragment of a title rather than a specification field. The cell shows the conflict rather than a resolution, and links to a numbered flag.

Unverified

No official source could be found within the scope of this review. The cell is left blank. It is never filled with an estimate, a range midpoint, an Amazon "typical price", or a figure from a third-party tool.

3. Fit score — what it is and what it is not

It is a weighted ranking of five factors that can each be traced to a source. It is not a demand forecast, a conversion prediction, or an earnings estimate. Amazon publishes no per-ASIN sales volume, search volume or conversion rate, and the third-party tools that guess at these produce estimates that cannot be reproduced or verified — so they are excluded (F-13).

4. Commission arithmetic

The published standard rate for the Kitchen category is 4.50% Amazon rate card ↗. That rate is public. The account-specific custom rate card is behind sign-in and was not read, so no personalised rate is claimed (F-05).

Assumption — price used for arithmetic Selling price used for arithmetic: $119.99 (the brand-store list price of the highest-scoring product, Chefman RJ38-8T-BLACK).
Commission per sale $5.40
119.99 × 4.50% = 5.3996 → $5.40 to the nearest cent (the rate card notes per-purchase rounding to the nearest local currency amount).
Assumption — units per month blank
Any traffic or conversion figure would be invented. The formula is published so the operator can plug in their own observed numbers from the Associates dashboard once live.

Which is why this site does not print a monthly earnings figure. The arithmetic is published so you can run it with your own observed numbers once traffic exists. Amazon itself states it makes no representation about achievable commission income Operating Agreement ↗.

Attribution windows that constrain the arithmetic

A click opens a 24-hour session. A cart completed within 89 days of the click still counts, and the order must ship and be paid for within 180 days of purchase Commission Statement ↗.

5. Relationship to the earlier build in this repository

Several agent sessions worked this repository concurrently. The consolidated canonical site is generated by build.py and verify.py at the repository root and is what the main Pages URL serves. This build is an annex published at site/. It does not modify the root site, its data, or its build scripts: it reads its own data from data/verified/ and writes only into site/. That separation is deliberate, so that two agents could each deliver without overwriting the other's work.

Three independent sessions resolved the same three opaque short links to the same three ASINs, which is a meaningful cross-check on the single most consequential fact in this project. The builds differ in depth rather than in conclusion: this one verifies manufacturer specifications field by field against the brands' own product and support pages, and it resolved the published commission rate, which earlier audits recorded as unreachable — Kitchen is a fixed 4.50% (F-17). The coexistence of a root site and this annex is recorded as F-16; if the annex data set is preferred, retiring it is a one-setting change to the Pages source.

python3 scripts/build_site.py   # regenerate every page from data/*.json
