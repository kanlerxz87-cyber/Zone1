What was checked and rejected

For completeness, three categories of source were considered and excluded:

  • Review-aggregator and price-history sites. They republish Amazon data without a licence and their figures cannot be traced to Amazon.
  • “Best air fryer” listicles and affiliate blogs. Circular: they cite each other and none is primary.
  • A third-party marketing site that stated the Emeril unit's wattage. The number may well be right, but a marketing site is not the manufacturer, so the field stays blank and is recorded as F-09.
