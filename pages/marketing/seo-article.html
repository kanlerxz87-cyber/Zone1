The short answer

If you want to cook two things at once and finish them at the same time, the 6-quart dual-basket format is the one to buy. If you want the simplest possible appliance for one cook at a time with a large basket, the 8-quart single-basket is the one. If you want a full cooking centre that replaces more than one appliance, the 26-quart French door oven is the one — and it costs materially more.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a dual basket?

Only if you regularly cook two things at different times and want them to finish together. For most households a single larger basket is simpler and cheaper.

Is a 26-quart air fryer oven worth it?

It is worth it if you cook for more than four people or host regularly. Below that, the capacity is unused and the price premium is real.

Are the baskets dishwasher safe?

The CHEFMAN 6 Quart dual basket has removable, non-stick coated dishwasher-safe baskets. The Chefman 8 Qt lists its basket and care instructions as dishwasher safe with a stainless steel inner material. The Emeril Lagasse 26QT care instructions could not be confirmed from the official page, so no claim is made for it. F-03

Bottom line

Buy the format your household actually cooks in. If that is two simultaneous dishes, the dual basket wins. If it is one dish with headroom, the 8-quart wins. If it is a full kitchen centre, the 26-quart French door wins — and it is the only one of the three that costs more than the others combined.
