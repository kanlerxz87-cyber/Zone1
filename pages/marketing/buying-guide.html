Feature glossary

Dual zone and Sync Finish

Dual-zone cooking means two independent baskets with their own temperature and time settings. Sync Finish is the part that matters: it holds one basket so that both finish at the same time instead of one finishing and going cold while the other catches up. Both terms appear in the manufacturer bullet list for CHEFMAN 6 Qt Dual Basket. Amazon

Hi-Fry

A higher maximum cooking temperature for crisper surfaces. The 6-quart model's bullet states a digital dual-basket range of 200–450 °F and lists Hi-Fry as a special feature. Amazon

Rapid-air technology

The air-circulation method named in Amazon's Features & Specs block for Chefman 8 Qt TurboFry 4-in-1. Amazon

Rotisserie

A motorised spit that rotates food while it cooks. It appears in the official title of Emeril Lagasse 26QT French Door. Amazon

Dual-speed fan

A fan with more than one speed setting, also named in the official title of Emeril Lagasse 26QT French Door. Amazon

LED shake reminder

A prompt to turn or shake the basket mid-cook, listed in the Features & Specs block for Chefman 8 Qt TurboFry 4-in-1. Amazon

Dehydrate and Frozen presets

Presets that extend an air fryer into a dehydrator or cook from frozen without a separate pre-thaw. Named in the official titles of Chefman 8 Qt TurboFry 4-in-1 and Emeril Lagasse 26QT French Door. Amazon
