What matters most

1. The supplied links were not product IDs

Each URL path segment looks like an ASIN but is an opaque redirect token. The links resolve to B0CDY277D3, B09B7SB46R and B08DKYBTPH — two brands, two product formats and a price band from roughly $80 to $150. Rebuilding links from the path segment would have pointed every future article at the wrong product. F-01

See the resolved link table →

2. Commission rates cannot be stated publicly

The Standard Commission Income schedule redirects to the Amazon sign-in page. The revenue model on the research page therefore takes the commission rate as an input the publisher supplies from their own rate card, and shows the arithmetic at a spread of illustrative rates. No rate is asserted as fact. F-04

See the commission model →

3. Two compliance gates must be closed before traffic

Program Policies allow a Site to show prices only if Amazon serves them with the link or they come from Creators API / PA API, and forbid displaying Amazon star ratings and reviews unless obtained through those APIs. This build is static research, so both gates are open and are published for review. F-07 F-08

Recommended sequence

  1. Close the two compliance gates. Replace the static price column with a SiteStripe / PA API widget and source ratings through PA API, or drop both from the published pages and keep them in the internal research record. This is a publisher decision — see F-07 and F-08 .
  2. Apply the Associates tag. Every link template currently carries the placeholder YOUR-TAG-XX. Replace it with the real Associates ID before any page is shared. F-11
  3. Stand up the re-verification job. Prices, badges and Best Sellers Rank are point-in-time. The schedule and the replacement path for live prices are specified on the Campaign & Tracking page. F-05
  4. Publish in order of certainty. Lead with P3 and P1, whose records are complete, and hold P2's specification-heavy comparison copy until its detail page serves a readable body again. F-03
  5. Keep the disclosure adjacent to every monetised block, not only in the footer, and never state a product experience the publisher has not had.
