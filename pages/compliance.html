Compliance

The rules this site is built against, quoted from the official Amazon Associates programme documents and the U.S. Federal Trade Commission's endorsement guidance. Every row links to the source it came from.

Two disclosures this site carries

Amazon — required statement

"As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases."

The Operating Agreement requires this to be stated clearly and prominently on your Site, and forbids misrepresenting or embellishing the relationship. It appears in the footer of every page of this site.

Source: Amazon Associates.

FTC — affiliate disclosure

"You should disclose your relationship to the retailer clearly and conspicuously on your site… You could say something like, 'I get commissions for purchases made through links in this post.'"

Placement matters: a disclosure only on an About page, only at the end of an article, or behind a "MORE" click is likely to be missed. Every monetised block in this project carries the disclosure immediately above it.

Source: FTC.

Wording that is not acceptable

WordingFTC assessmentOfficial source
"Affiliate link"Not adequate on its own — consumers might not understand that the person placing the link is paid for purchases through it. FTC
"Buy now" button (as the disclosure)Not adequate. FTC
"Paid link" next to an affiliate linkAdequate disclosure of the nature of the link.FTC
"Commissionable link"Probably not a clear disclosure. FTC
"Thanks to the brand for the free product"Acceptable when it makes clear what you received and from whom.FTC
"#ad", "Ad:", "advertisement", "sponsored"Effective; place it where it is hard to miss and near the recommendation.FTC

Automation rules

IDRuleDetailOfficial source
R-01Never auto-purchase through your own Special LinksSelf-purchase is prohibited and would also void the commission. Any automated QA that clicks links must use an account that is not linked to your tag, and must not place orders.Amazon Associates
R-02Respect robots.txt and do not scrape Program Content at scaleAmazon's robots.txt disallows a large set of paths for all crawlers and explicitly disallows all paths for a long list of AI and research agents (for example GPTBot, ClaudeBot, ChatGPT-User, PerplexityBot, Google-Extended, CCBot, Scrapy, and others). Automated product research in this project must therefore use the official Creators API / PA API rather than harvesting rendered pages, and any AI training or fine-tuning on Program Content is prohibited.amazon.com
R-03Refresh price and badge claims before publishingLimited-time promotion references must be removed as soon as the promotion ends, and prices may only be shown when served by Amazon or obtained through Creators API / PA API. Build a scheduled re-verification job that re-reads the official pages and re-publishes the catalog with a new verification timestamp, or replace the static price column with a live SiteStripe/PA API widget.Amazon Associates
R-04Use per-campaign sub-tags for measurementAmazon may issue additional "sub-tag" Associate IDs on request so that link performance can be monitored and optimised per placement. Sub-tags must not be assigned dynamically to individual end users.Amazon Associates
R-05Keep disclosure adjacent to every monetised recommendationAutomated content generation must place the FTC-style affiliate disclosure and the Amazon statement "As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases." at the top of the monetised block, not only in a site-wide footer.FTC
R-06Do not use link shortening or redirecting wrappersEvery outbound Amazon link must be a direct amazon.com URL carrying the Associates tag parameter. Redirect chains, cloaking and intermediate redirect pages void commission and risk account termination.Amazon Associates
R-07No price alerts and no price trackerThe site must not implement price tracking or price alerting functionality unless Amazon has agreed otherwise in writing.Amazon Associates
R-08Gate any AI-assisted scoring behind a human approval stepBecause claims about a Product must not be inaccurate, overbroad, deceptive or misleading, and because an endorser may not describe an experience they have not had, AI-generated copy must be reviewed against the verified product record before publication, and must not assert first-hand experience.Amazon Associates
