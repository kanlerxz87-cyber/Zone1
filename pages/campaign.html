3. Measurement stack

What Amazon measures for you

  • Clicks. Every Special Link click is tracked and reported by Amazon.
  • Conversions. A Qualifying Purchase is a click that turns into a paid, shipped, non-returned order inside the 24-hour Session window, with cart-to-order completion allowed up to 89 days after the click and shipment and payment within 180 days of the purchase.
  • Commission. Reported monthly, paid approximately 60 days after the end of the calendar month in which it was earned.
  • Disqualification. Cancelled, returned or refunded orders are removed from commission; so are purchases attributed to a Prohibited Paid Search Placement, a search-engine-generated link, or a Redirecting Link.

Source: Amazon Associates.

What you must measure yourself

  • Impressions and on-site engagement per placement, joined to Amazon's click and order data on the sub-tag.
  • EPC per placement (commission ÷ clicks) — the only metric that should drive content decisions, since Amazon's conversion rate is not published per link.
  • Content freshness: how long since each page's price and badge claims were last re-verified.
  • Compliance state per page: whether the affiliate disclosure is present and adjacent to the monetised block, and whether the Amazon statement appears.

Use a privacy-respecting, first-party analytics setup. Do not fingerprint or track individual users — and note that you may not assign sub-tags dynamically to individual end users.

5. Optimisation loop

1 · Measure

Weekly: clicks, orders and EPC per sub-tag from the Amazon report, joined to on-site engagement.

2 · Diagnose

Separate a low EPC caused by low CTR (a headline or placement problem) from one caused by low conversion (a price, rating or offer problem).

3 · Change one thing

Test one variable per placement per cycle — headline, placement, CTA label, or which product the CTA points at. Keep the sub-tag stable so the test is readable.

4 · Re-verify

Before any change ships, re-read the cited official pages. If a price or badge has moved, update it or remove it in the same commit.
