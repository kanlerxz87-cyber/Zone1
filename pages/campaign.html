Optimization loop

  1. Re-fetch every price and rating on the schedule in Niche & trend; expire anything past its window (pipeline/rot_sweep.py).
  2. Prune finished promotions automatically (pipeline/promo_expiry.py) — an expired discount claim is a policy breach, not a typo.
  3. Retire pages whose product left the catalog (pipeline/eligibility.py).
  4. Re-run platform/audit/report.py; the site does not rebuild if a row loses its source.

Steps 1–3 need the credentials in Automation; step 4 runs today and is wired into CI.
