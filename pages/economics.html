Economics & commission

How a click becomes money, per the official Commission Income Statement — with the rate left as an unset input.

Advertising disclosure. As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases. This site is a demonstration platform; it currently publishes no live affiliate links.

The formula, with its missing input visible

commission = (gross_charged − shipping − gift_wrapping − handling − taxes − service_charges − credits − rebates − card_processing − bad_debt) × rate_pct / 100

Guard. model/economics.py raises RateNotAvailable rather than defaulting to a plausible-looking percentage. A revenue model built on an assumed rate is how affiliate dashboards end up reporting money that does not exist.
Risk note. Amazon states it makes no representation about the traffic or commission income you can expect, and may cease all commission income on a breach — so the platform treats compliance as the primary control, not as a checklist afterthought.
