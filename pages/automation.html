Credential contract

  • AMAZON_ASSOCIATE_TAG — the tagged link suffix Amazon assigned to your account
  • AMAZON_PA_ACCESS_KEY / AMAZON_PA_SECRET_KEY — from Associates Central → Tools → Product Advertising API
  • AMAZON_PA_PARTNER_TYPE — the partner type Amazon issued with those credentials

Credentials are read from the environment only. Absent credentials put a stage into skipped-no-credentials. There is no code path that simulates a successful fetch, because simulated data on this site would be indistinguishable from research.

Non-goals

  • No scraping of Amazon pages to replace the API; Program Content may only be obtained through the approved channels.
  • No training on Program Content or Special Links.
  • No simulated earnings, clicks or conversions when an account is absent.
  • No auto-published content: drafts go through the claim checker and a human review step before they reach the site.
