F-01The supplied link.amazon path segments are not ASINs
Link resolution
raised by zone1-main
also logged as d14c5409 F04
highEach supplied URL looks like it contains an ASIN in its path: link.amazon/B04G1MEwY, link.amazon/B02NbDxp3 and link.amazon/B00hJ57DV. Requesting them over HTTP resolves to three unrelated product pages: /dp/B0CDY277D3 (CHEFMAN 6 Quart Dual Basket Air Fryer Oven), /dp/B09B7SB46R (Emeril Lagasse 26QT French Door Air Fryer Toaster Oven) and /dp/B08DKYBTPH (Chefman Air Fryer 8 Qt TurboFry). The path segment is therefore an opaque redirect token, not the ASIN of the product being promoted.Rebuilding the links from the ASIN-looking segment would point every future article at the wrong product. All links in this project are rebuilt from the resolved /dp/<ASIN> destination plus the publisher's tag parameter.Resolved each link, recorded the final URL and the destination page <title> in data/products.json, and rebuilt all outbound links from the resolved ASIN.link.amazon · link.amazon · link.amazon · Amazon · Amazon · Amazonneeds review
F-02Amazon renders the used-condition offer without labelling its condition inline
Price presentation
raised by zone1-main
also logged as d14c5409 F01 (superseded)
mediumAmazon's buy box for B0CDY277D3 carries exactly two offers. The embedded buy-box payload lists $79.93 with buyingOptionType NEW and $57.45 with buyingOptionType USED. In the rendered price block the second offer appears as "$57.45 with 43 percent savings -43%" and "$28.73 per count", followed by the same "List Price: $99.99" line and the list-price explanatory note about "the suggested retail price of a new product". The condition word USED appears only in the embedded payload, not in the rendered text next to the price, so a reader scanning the page — or a scraper reading the rendered text alone — can reasonably read $57.45 as a second new-condition price. The two offers are also not stable between fetches: the same URL has served different buy-box contents across retrieval windows.Publishing $57.45 as a new-condition price would be a false statement about the product and would breach the FTC truthfulness rule for endorsements. Price volatility matters for the same reason: any price quoted on the site is a dated snapshot that must be re-verified before it is republished.The catalogue publishes $79.93 as the new-condition price and $57.45 as the used-condition offer, and states the condition tag Amazon itself applies, with the exact URL it was read from. The misleading second-new-price row was removed from the dataset. The published price snapshot is dated and carries a re-verification schedule on the Campaign page, and the site's standing caveat says plainly that these are dated snapshots of official pages rather than licensed Product Advertising Content.Amazon · Amazonneeds review
