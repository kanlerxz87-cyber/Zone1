F-01 · link.amazon is not a citable affiliate link format high

The supplied host is Amazon's internal mobile-link service, not the Associates linking tool. The program requires the tagged Special Link format carrying the publisher's own Associates ID as a URL parameter, and disqualifies purchases reached through a link that bounces the user to Amazon via an intermediate site without an affirmative click. A platform that stored and republished 'link.amazon/...' strings would earn nothing on the traffic it drove and would be one step from a cloaking violation.

EVIDENCE
  • Special Links must use the assigned Associates ID and appear as XXXXX-## in the URL parameter.
  • 'any Product purchased by a customer who is referred to an Amazon Site by a link that sends users indirectly to an Amazon Site via an intermediate site, without requiring the customer to click on a link or take some other affirmative action on that intermediate site' is a disqualified purchase.
  • 'All Special Links must be accessed directly from your Site.'
SOURCES FOR MANUAL REVIEW
  • Associates Program Policies — Participation Requirements §2 (Links on Your Site)

Action required: Rebuild every link from the operator's own Associates ID using SiteStripe or the Associates dashboard; never republish a third-party short link. needs review

F-11 · 'Track clicks and conversions' has no first-party click data source medium

Amazon reports clicks and conversions in its own dashboard; a publisher cannot lawfully reconstruct per-user click tracking, and tying a sub-tag to an identified visitor is explicitly forbidden.
