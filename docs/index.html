Executive summary

  • What this is. Three product links were supplied. None resolves to a product: the identifiers are 9 characters (ASINs are 10) and each canonical check returns Amazon's page-not-found (S13–S15). The link.amazon redirects land on Amazon's missing-page fallback (random listings) — see flag F17.
  • What is verified. Nothing product-level. All identity fields (title, brand, capacity, department, category) are blank and marked unverified because the mapping from submitted code to product is disputed (F17). The official commission rate table is verified (S16).
  • What is not verified. Product identity, price, rating, review count and per-product commission rate. These are left blank. No pricing, review or commission analysis is provided.
  • Compliance. None of the supplied links carry an Associates tag, and the Associates account is not confirmed. The required disclosure is shown with a pending status. See Compliance and Flagged for Review.
  • Not built. The full AI platform (automated product discovery, trend and niche detection, competitor analysis, article generation at scale, click and conversion tracking) needs Amazon API and account data that is not available here. No data was invented to fill that gap (flag F11).
  • Next step for review. Work through the Flagged for Review list. High-severity items F01, F03, F06, F11 and F17 need an owner decision.
