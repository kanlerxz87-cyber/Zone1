What the specifications actually mean in a kitchen

Two baskets beat one bigger basket only if you use them independently. The claimed advantage is Sync Finish: you set the two baskets differently and the machine times them to land together S7. If you mostly cook one thing, an 8 Qt single basket gives you more room for the same money.

90–400 °F is a wider range than most basket fryers publish, and the low end is the interesting part. At 90 °F the dehydrate function is usable; at 400 °F it is a normal air fryer. It does not reach the 450 °F that Chefman's own 8 Qt model advertises.

1700 W is a real circuit consideration. On a 120 V North American circuit that is close to a 15 A breaker's practical ceiling, so do not plan to run it on the same circuit as a kettle.

Verdict, stated as a condition rather than a score

Buy this one if your recurring problem is "the fries and the chicken need different times and temperatures". Do not buy it if you need one large basket, or if you need oven-style baking and rotisserie — the 26 Qt French-door oven is a different machine for that job.
