How commission income is calculated (official method, no rate applied)

Standard Commission Income is a percentage of Qualifying Revenue. Qualifying Revenue is the amount Amazon receives, less shipping, gift wrap, handling fees, taxes, service charges, credits, rebates, card processing fees and bad debt (Commission Income Statement s.1 and s.3). The rate is set per category on the schedule that is not reachable (S9). No amount has been calculated for any product.
