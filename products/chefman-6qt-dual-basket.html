A useful way to decide

Start with the two separate 3-quart baskets and the documented Sync Finish behaviour. They may suit two dishes with different settings, but a combined capacity figure does not tell you whether one large item fits. Confirm the dual-window model before using its manual; the 450 °F Hi-Fry figure describes a two-minute mode, not a promise about a full cooking cycle. S18

This is a document-based decision rule, not a tested verdict. We cannot rate food quality, noise, durability or value for money from these sources. No star score is assigned.
