Verdict, limited to what is verified

Two independently controlled 3-quart baskets make this a format to consider when you want separate settings for two dishes. The manual distinguishes Sync Baskets from Sync Finish and describes Hi-Fry as a 450°F finish for two minutes, not a full-cycle setting. That is a documented feature set, not a claim about food quality or how many people it will feed. Check the exact model and unresolved link intent before a purchase. S10 S18
