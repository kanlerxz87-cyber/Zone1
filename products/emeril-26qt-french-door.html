A useful way to decide

Start with the oven-style cavity, the documented modes and the space the manual requires. If rotisserie or toast is the reason for choosing it, check the exact model, accessories and mode instructions before buying. The preset-count and model-code conflicts remain unresolved; a larger quoted capacity is not evidence of better cooking or useful tray area. S14 S15

This is a document-based decision rule, not a tested verdict. We cannot rate food quality, noise, durability or value for money from these sources. No star score is assigned.
