A useful way to decide

Start with the single-basket format, then stop at the model conflict: the documented RJ38-SQSS-8T range is 200–400 °F, while the observed Amazon title advertises a 450 °F Hi-Fry option. A separate Chefman SKU also advertises Hi-Fry, but similarity does not identify the unit supplied by an ASIN. Ask for the exact model and its manual rather than treating either temperature claim as settled. S12 S13 S29

This is a document-based decision rule, not a tested verdict. We cannot rate food quality, noise, durability or value for money from these sources. No star score is assigned.
