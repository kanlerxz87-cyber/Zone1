Verdict, limited to what is verified

The RJ38-SQSS-8T brand page and manual document an 8-quart single basket, a 200–400°F range and four food-type presets. Recorded listing evidence carries conflicting 450°F/Hi-Fry wording; a separate Chefman model page does not prove what will ship from that listing.
