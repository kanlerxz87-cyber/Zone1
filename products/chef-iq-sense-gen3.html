CHEF iQ Sense Gen3 Wireless Meat Thermometer

A rechargeable wireless probe and a hub that relays readings to your phone. The manufacturer publishes a 32–212 °F internal range, a 1,000 °F ambient rating and a 70-hour battery figure. S39

What it is for: Watching a long cook without standing at the oven, and seeing both the food’s internal temperature and the surrounding ambient heat. The manufacturer states the probe also works in an air fryer, an oven, a grill, a smoker, a deep fryer and sous vide. S39

Check first: The brand page sells one to four probes at different prices and the default selection on the day read was a single probe. Count the probes you are actually buying, and note that wireless monitoring depends on the Smart Hub and the app, not the probe alone. S39

The short version · editorial guidance

This is a connected-cooking tool, and the honest question is whether you want that. The wireless probe and hub let you watch a roast from another room and read ambient heat as well as the centre of the food, which is genuinely different from a $20 instant-read. The constraints are equally concrete: the internal range stops at 212 °F, the app and hub are part of the product, and the probe count you receive depends on the configuration you buy. Compare it to a simple instant-read on the features you would actually use, not on the number of sensors. S39

What owning it is like.

Care: The brand states the probe is waterproof and dishwasher safe. Charge through the hub with the included USB-C cable; the brand says no charging brick is included, so plan for one you already own. S39

A thermometer answers one question: has the food reached a safe internal temperature. USDA publishes the minimums (145 °F for whole cuts with a three-minute rest, 160 °F for ground meat, 165 °F for poultry and leftovers) and advises cooking to them as measured with a food thermometer. S48 S49
