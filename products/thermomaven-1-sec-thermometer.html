Why the rate is left blank

Amazon's rate table assigns a rate by the category of the item bought, and it does not publish which category it applies to a given ASIN. The observed breadcrumb for this ASIN is Home & Kitchen > Kitchen & Dining > Kitchen Utensils & Gadgets > Thermometers & Timers > Meat, a different path from the three air-fryer listings, so the settled category cannot be assumed to be the same. Only an Associates Earnings report names the category on a shipped line. S3

This site sets no cookies itself, runs no analytics, and has no comment forms or accounts.
