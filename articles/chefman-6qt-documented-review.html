Chefman 6 Qt Dual Basket: what the documents establish

Two drawers, separate settings and a short Hi-Fry finish. This is a guide to model RJ38-SQPF-3TDB-2W, not a report from a test kitchen.

The documented format

The manufacturer lists 6 Qt / 5.67 L split into two 3-quart baskets. The body is listed at 13.38 × 11.81 × 10.63 in / 33.98 × 30 × 27 cm and 13.3 lb / 6.03 kg.

Those are manufacturer specifications. They do not measure usable food capacity, noise, cooking evenness or how comfortable the baskets are to handle.

Sources for this section: S10 S18

The useful feature is the schedule

Sync Finish delays the shorter cook so the two timers finish together. Sync Baskets copies the first basket’s settings to the second. Pulling a basket during a sync cook pauses both; the manual also explains which adjustments break the sync.

Editorial judgement: this layout is worth considering if separate settings solve a real meal-planning problem. It is not automatically preferable for one large item.

Sources for this section: S18

Do not read Hi-Fry as a whole-cycle setting

The product page states 200–450 °F / 95–235 °C. The manual describes the Hi-Fry button as 450 °F / 235 °C, held for 2 minutes, for extra browning. Neither passage establishes a proofing or dehydrating function at the lower end.

Sources for this section: S10 S18

Care and identity checks before a purchase

The manual prohibits aerosol cooking-oil spray on the baskets and racks, permits a non-aerosol mister and says cooled baskets and racks are top-rack dishwasher safe. Follow the complete instructions, including cooling and non-submersion warnings.

This guide describes the named manufacturer model. It does not certify that the submitted short link supplies it. Confirm the selected listing, model and accessories before relying on these specifications.
