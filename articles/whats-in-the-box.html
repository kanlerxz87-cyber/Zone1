What ships in the box

Seven accessories, a six-accessory title, and one honest caveat

Two of Emeril's own documents say 7 accessories. Amazon's title for the same listing says six. The owner's manual settles the count and then takes it back — here is what each document actually says.

Zone1 Kitchen · AI-assisted· Document check· 5 min read· sources read 2026-11-14· Documentation-based — no hands-on testing Cluster: What do the numbers actually mean?
No hands-on testing, and no Amazon numbers

Nobody here cooked with these appliances. Every specification below is quoted from the manufacturer's own product page or owner's manual and links to it. No Amazon price, star rating or review count appears, because Amazon's Program Policies only permit those when Amazon serves the link or via Creators/PA API S2.

  • French Door AirFryer 360 Toaster Oven Combo, 26 QT — Kitchen appliances → Air fryer toaster ovens
  • TurboFry Touch 6 Qt. Dual-Basket / Dual-Window Air Fryer — Kitchen appliances → Air fryers
  • Air Fryer 8 Qt — TurboFry 4-in-1 (stainless steel) — Kitchen appliances → Air fryers

Two brand documents say seven. The Amazon title says six.

The Emeril product page states, in one sentence: "Included are 7 premium accessories to maximize versatility—air fry basket, rotisserie spit, bake pan, and more."

The Amazon listing title for the same product ends with "Six Cooking Accessories Included" — one fewer than the brand's own count. The full title is quoted on the product's data page, and the disagreement is recorded in that product's flag register rather than resolved by picking a winner. This site publishes the count the two brand documents agree on.

The owner's manual takes the third, most useful approach: it enumerates thirteen numbered parts. Six are built into the appliance (main unit, door handle, glass door, LED display, control panel, control knob), which leaves 7 removable accessories: a drip tray, a wire rack, a baking pan, a rotisserie spit, a crisper tray, a rotisserie fetch tool and a plate handle.

Sources for this section: S14 S28 S15

The manual's caveat is the sentence that matters

Immediately after its parts list, the manual says: "Some accessories may not be included with purchase." That is the manufacturer declining to guarantee its own accessory count — and it is the one line almost no retail listing quotes.

Judgement: for a buyer who needs a specific accessory — the rotisserie spit for a recipe, the bake pan for a family meal — the count is trivia and the caveat is the fact. Check the box on delivery day; the documents give no promise to enforce.

Sources for this section: S15

The Chefman documents answer a different question

Neither Chefman unit publishes an accessory count at all. The 6 Qt dual-basket's care row reads: Nonstick, dishwasher-safe baskets. Manual caution: never spray with aerosol cooking oil — use a non-aerosol mister instead. Baskets and racks, described by coating and cleaning rather than by a parts list.

The 8 Qt TurboFry's documents name its removable parts as: Nonstick basket and rack; top-rack dishwasher safe — and stop there. Its brand page sells the machine around them: Fries, Chicken, Fish, Meat (one-touch) presets, an LED shake reminder, and designed for 4–6 people as the household pitch.

So the honest cross-brand comparison is not seven versus nothing. It is that one manufacturer documents a box list with a caveat, and the other two publish no count anywhere this site could verify — a gap, which is left visible here rather than filled from a third-party seller's guess.
