Dual-basket or countertop oven? The 8-quart question nobody answers

Two 3-quart drawers and one 26-quart cavity solve opposite problems. Here is the documented difference, and the one measurement that decides it for your kitchen.

The two machines are not competing for the same job

A dual-basket air fryer is one appliance pretending to be two. The Chefman 6 Qt splits a 6 Qt / 5.67 L cavity into Two 3-quart baskets and gives each its own time and temperature, then adds Sync Finish so the shorter cook is delayed to land with the longer one.

A countertop oven is a different shape of compromise: one big cavity, many modes. The Emeril 26 QT is documented at 26 qt / 24.6 L (manual: 1,519 cu in) with a dual-speed fan, a rotisserie, and French doors.

Sources for this section: S10 S18 S14

What you can actually cook, stated in the manufacturer's own numbers

The dual-basket unit's ceiling is 450 °F / 235 °C, held for 2 minutes, for extra browning, over a 200–450 °F / 95–235 °C range.

The oven's documented range is 75–500 °F / 25–260 °C on 120 V / 60 Hz at a rated 1700 W. It is the only unit here that goes below 200 °F, which is what makes proofing and dehydrating possible rather than nominal.

What neither page states is how evenly either one cooks. That number does not exist on a manufacturer page, and this site does not invent it.

Sources for this section: S10 S18 S15

The measurement that decides it

Footprint, not capacity. The dual-basket unit is 13.38 × 11.81 × 10.63 in / 33.98 × 30 × 27 cm and weighs 13.3 lb / 6.03 kg. The oven is 16.9 D × 15.8 W × 14.8 H in.

Put a tape measure on the counter and add the clearance the manual asks for behind and above the unit. A 26-quart oven that does not fit under your cabinets is not a 26-quart oven; it is a return.

Sources for this section: S10 S14

The honest summary

Buy the dual-basket if you cook two things at two temperatures and want them to finish together. Buy the oven if you want one cavity that can also toast, proof and rotisserie, and you have measured the space.

If you want a single large basket instead, the third product in this set is documented as 8 qt (brand also sells a 5 qt version of the same product page) and Designed for 4–6 people — but read that product's own page before you buy, because its Amazon listing and its brand page disagree about the temperature ceiling.
