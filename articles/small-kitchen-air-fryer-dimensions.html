Fit check

Will it actually fit? Three air fryers, measured against your counter

Every manufacturer on this site publishes outer dimensions. That turns 'will it fit' from a guess into arithmetic — and it is the one buying question a spec sheet can answer completely.

No hands-on testing, and no Amazon numbers

Nobody here cooked with these appliances. Every specification below is quoted from the manufacturer's own product page or owner's manual and links to it. The number you measure first is not the width

Most counter-space failures are depth or height failures, because people measure the width of the gap they can see and ignore the distance the appliance needs in front of it. The Emeril is documented at 16.9 D × 15.8 W × 14.8 H in, and its French doors swing forward rather than up, so the space in front of it matters as much as the space under the cabinet.

The two Chefman units are both shorter and shallower on paper. The dual-window 6 Qt is documented at 13.38 × 11.81 × 10.63 in / 33.98 × 30 × 27 cm. The 8 Qt's published row reads 8 Qt model: 15.4 × 12.6 × 13.5 in / 39.11 × 32 × 34.29 cm · 12.8 lb / 5.8 kg. Read the manual's own clearance instructions before deciding — a published outer dimension is not a published clearance.

Weight matters when you lift it out to clean

These are not built-in appliances. Every one of them gets lifted, wiped and put back, so weight is a daily detail rather than a shipping detail. The manufacturer's own figure for the 6 Qt dual-window is 13.3 lb / 6.03 kg. The 8 Qt publishes its weight on the same line as its dimensions — 8 Qt model: 15.4 × 12.6 × 13.5 in / 39.11 × 32 × 34.29 cm · 12.8 lb / 5.8 kg — and the Emeril's specification block does not state a weight.

If the appliance is going above shoulder height or into a cupboard, that is the number to weigh, not the capacity.

Sources for this section: S10 S12 S14

Capacity per square inch of counter

The interesting ratio is how much cooking volume each unit buys per unit of counter it occupies. The 6 Qt dual-window gives two 3-quart baskets inside a footprint of roughly 158 square inches. The 8 Qt single basket gives 8 qt (brand also sells a 5 qt version of the same product page) on a slightly larger footprint. The Emeril gives 26 qt / 24.6 L (manual: 1,519 cu in) on the largest footprint of the three.

That ratio is arithmetic on published numbers, not a measurement of usable volume: a manufacturer's quart figure and a usable cooking area are not the same thing, and no official source read for this site states usable interior area.

Sources for this section: S10 S12 S14

What this page cannot tell you

It cannot tell you the clearance your cabinet manufacturer requires, the heat your countertop tolerates, or how loud the fan is — none of that is published by these brands in the pages read for this site. Measure the gap, read the manual's clearance section, and treat the published outer dimensions as the starting point rather than the answer.
