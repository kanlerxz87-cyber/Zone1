Fit check

Will an air fryer fit on your counter? Measure these things first

The footprint is only the beginning. Leave space to open it, follow the ventilation instructions and check where the cord will reach.

By Zone1 Kitchen · AI-assisted, document-based writing· 3 min read· Sources checked 2026-11-19· Updated 19 Nov 2026 Documentation-based — no hands-on testing Cluster: Which machine should I buy?

No appliance was tested for this guide. Specifications are attributed to their documents; editorial guidance is not a cooking result. How we research · first-publication timestamp is not established.

Mark out the published footprint

The Chefman dual-window page gives 13.38 × 11.81 × 10.63 in / 33.98 × 30 × 27 cm. For the 8 Qt model, Chefman’s specification block gives 8 Qt model: 15.4 × 12.6 × 13.5 in / 39.11 × 32 × 34.29 cm · 12.8 lb / 5.8 kg. The Emeril page gives 16.9 D × 15.8 W × 14.8 H in.

Use the source’s stated orientation; do not silently reorder an unlabelled dimension string. Mark a footprint on the counter as a planning aid, then leave extra working space for opening and handling the appliance. The brand’s dimensions are not a measurement made by this publication.

Sources for this section: Chefman 6 Qt dual-window page Chefman 8 Qt TurboFry page Emeril 26 Qt product page

Add the model-specific ventilation space

The 8 Qt Chefman manual asks for at least 6 inches of clearance on the top and back. The Emeril manual asks for at least 5 inches at the top, back and sides and says not to operate in an enclosed space or under hanging cabinets. A machine fitting on a shelf does not establish that it can be used there.

Keep the instructions separate. Do not apply the oven’s five-inch figure to a different model, or treat a single clearance number as permission to ignore the rest of a manual’s placement rules.

Sources for this section: Chefman RJ38-SQSS-8T guide Emeril FAFO-001 manual

Think about opening, cleaning and power

Leave a safe place to put a removed basket or accessory and room to reach the appliance without crowding it. This is a planning suggestion, not a measured opening clearance for any of these models.

Check the exact unit’s electrical rating and current instructions. The Emeril PDF itself contains conflicting extension-cord language: its safeguards say never use one, while a later power-cord section describes conditional use. That conflict is not permission to use an extension cord; ask the manufacturer to clarify before relying on it.

Sources for this section: Emeril FAFO-001 manual Chefman RJ38-SQSS-8T guide Chefman RJ38-SQPF-3TDB-2W guide

Before you order

Record the model number, the space available and the particular manual you checked. Recheck the listing before buying: the 8 Qt listing title’s 450 °F Hi-Fry wording does not match the 200–400 °F range on the RJ38-SQSS-8T brand page. Similar names do not make interchangeable specifications.

Sources for this section: Chefman 8 Qt TurboFry page Amazon listing B08DKYBTPH

Documentation-based guidance, not a hands-on test. Match the exact model and read the full current instructions before buying or using it.
