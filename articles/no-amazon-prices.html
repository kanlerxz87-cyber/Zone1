Why this site looks like this

Why this site does not show live Amazon prices or ratings

There are permitted programme routes for some Amazon content. This publication has not configured an authorised integration, and it does not substitute scraped values.

By Zone1 Kitchen · AI-assisted, document-based writing· 3 min read· Sources checked 2026-11-19· Updated 19 Nov 2026 Documentation-based — no hands-on testing Cluster: Can this page be trusted with my money?

No appliance was tested for this guide. Specifications are attributed to their documents; editorial guidance is not a cooking result. How we research · first-publication timestamp is not established.

The price rule is conditional—not a universal ban

Amazon’s Program Policies §2(b) condition price and availability display on Amazon serving the link with that data, or obtaining the data through the specified authorised APIs and complying with their licence. This site does neither.

Customer reviews and star ratings have a separate rule in §6(t), tied to the specified API route and licence. A price clause should not be cited as though it were the review clause, or vice versa.

What is absent here

There is no live Amazon price, availability indicator, star rating or scraped review summary in this publication. Following a clearly identified Amazon link lets a reader check the listing’s current configuration and terms.

A dated manufacturer list-price snapshot or brand-widget observation is labelled as the brand’s own content. It is not an Amazon quote, a current offer or a basis for a performance ranking.

Sources for this section: S2 S10 S12 S14

Price tracking is another permission question

The policies separately restrict price-tracking/alert functionality unless Amazon agrees. This site does not run a price history, deal timer, price alert or “lowest ever” badge.

If an authorised content integration becomes available later, its current licence, display, freshness and permitted-use conditions need their own review. An affiliate disclosure does not grant those permissions.

Sources for this section: S2 S1

The disclosure still matters

The required Associate sentence appears just below the masthead and again in the footer. Main product links repeat the affiliate relationship and the unconfirmed intended-listing/attribution boundary.

FTC guidance says disclosures should be hard to miss and with the endorsement. No particular placement here is described as an Amazon or FTC certification.

Sources for this section: S1 S5 S8

Privacy, local drafts & hosting
