Who pays for an affiliate buying guide? Read the disclosure

Affiliate funding should be visible, and it should not be confused with product experience. Here is what this publication says—and does not claim.

The financial relationship

Amazon requires the Associate identification sentence. It appears just below this site’s masthead and repeats in the footer. The main outbound product CTAs also identify the affiliate relationship near the link.

The possibility of a commission is not a statement that any click or purchase here has been credited. Intended listings and enrolled-account attribution remain unconfirmed; no earnings report is held.

A byline is not a test result

This publication identifies itself as Zone1 Kitchen and discloses AI-assisted document research. No named human editor or personal product experience has been established.

FTC guidance says not to describe experience with a product you have not tried. That is why these guides do not narrate cooking results, invent customer quotes or attach a test score to specifications.

What the guides can offer

A guide can help compare documented layouts, capacities, dimensions and care instructions, while leaving conflicts visible. It cannot manufacture the experience of owning or cooking in the appliance.

No live Amazon price/rating integration is configured. A dated manufacturer snapshot is labelled as such, and disclosure alone does not grant permission to scrape or reuse Amazon content.
