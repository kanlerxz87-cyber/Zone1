How the money works

Who pays for the air-fryer guide you are reading?

An affiliate link creates a potential financial connection. Here is what this publication discloses—and what its current account evidence does not establish.

The connection should be visible before the click

Amazon’s Operating Agreement requires the Associate identification statement. The FTC’s disclosure guidance says a material connection should be made clear and hard to miss, with the endorsement rather than only on an About page.

This site places the prescribed sentence beneath the masthead and repeats it in the footer. Product-link copy also identifies the affiliate relationship. That layout is not a certificate that every legal or programme requirement has been satisfied.

Sources for this section: S1 S8

Potential commission is not a reported earning

The programme describes commission for qualifying purchases subject to its terms. This repository has no authorised report establishing earnings, orders or successful attribution for these links.

The selected tracking ID is recorded but not verified active. The campaign ledger is empty. A click on this site would not, on its own, establish an Amazon order.

Sources for this section: S1

No test kitchen is being implied

The FTC guidance says not to describe experience with a product you have not tried. These guides read manufacturer documents; no appliance was bought, unboxed or cooked in for this publication.

An original diagram or an AI-generated food scene is not a product photograph or proof of an outcome. The visual labels and editorial-method page explain that distinction.

Sources for this section: S8

What to look for in any guide

Editorial checklist: a visible financial disclosure, a clear account of how the material was made, sources next to factual claims and an honest description of the limits. Treat a documented feature, an author’s judgement and a measured result as different kinds of statement.

Privacy, cookies & local-only decision storage

This site sets no cookies itself, runs no analytics, and has no public accounts, comment forms or email-signup form. The analytics client is implemented but is not loaded: no production collector is deployed. No production event counts have been measured by this site.

The owner-only decision picker stores choices in this browser’s local storage. It sends nothing automatically; choices leave the page only when explicitly copied or downloaded. Use its clear button to remove the saved choices.

Hosting is separate: GitHub states that Pages logs visitors’ IP addresses for security. Any future analytics host or proxy also needs its own logging and retention review. Following an Amazon link takes you to Amazon, where Amazon’s privacy notice and cookie practices apply.
