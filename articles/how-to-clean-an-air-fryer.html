Care and upkeep

How do you clean an air fryer? Start with the removable parts

“Dishwasher safe” does not mean the whole appliance. The two Chefman manuals and the Emeril manual describe different care routines.

By Zone1 Kitchen · AI-assisted, document-based writing· 3 min read· Sources checked 2026-11-19· Updated 19 Nov 2026 Documentation-based — no hands-on testing Cluster: What is it like to live with?

No appliance was tested for this guide. Specifications are attributed to their documents; editorial guidance is not a cooking result. How we research · first-publication timestamp is not established.

Unplug it and let it cool first

All three manuals tell you to disconnect the appliance and let it cool before cleaning. Do not turn a cleaning checklist into permission to handle a hot rack or basket. The Chefman manuals explicitly warn against lifting a rack out while it is hot.

The body of the appliance is not a dishwasher item. Both Chefman manuals say not to put the unit in a dishwasher or submerge it; Emeril likewise prohibits soaking or dishwashing the appliance.

Sources for this section: S18 S13 S15

For the two Chefman models: baskets and racks

Chefman’s manuals recommend washing the removable basket or baskets and racks with warm, soapy water, then rinsing and drying well. They also permit those parts on the top rack of a dishwasher. That statement applies to the documented parts and models, not every accessory sold for an air fryer.

Use a damp cloth on the cooled body and avoid abrasive cleaners. The dual-window manual says not to use window cleaner on its windows. Both manuals warn against aerosol cooking spray on the nonstick parts; they distinguish that from a non-aerosol oil mister.

Sources for this section: S18 S13

For the Emeril oven: plan for accessories and the cavity

The Emeril cleaning section recommends hand-washing accessories, soaking caked-on food in warm, soapy water when needed, and using nonabrasive cleaning tools. It tells you not to scrub the fragile heating coils and not to leave standing water inside the appliance.

Its instructions are not the same as the Chefman top-rack permission. Do not describe all three as dishwasher-safe appliances, and do not assume one care routine applies to an oven cavity, its doors and every removable part.

Sources for this section: S15 S18 S13

Use the care routine as a buying question

Before choosing, think about where the cooled removable parts will be washed and dried, and whether the documented routine fits your habits. This publication has not timed cleanup or tested a coating. The manual can tell you what is permitted; it cannot tell you whether you will enjoy doing it.
