Model watch

Two 8-quart Chefman models, and a listing identity still to confirm

The stainless Touch model and the matte-black High Fry model have different documented controls. A similar product name does not make them interchangeable.

The stainless model’s documents

Chefman’s RJ38-SQSS-8T product page states 200–400 °F and names food presets. The matching manual identifies the same model and describes a single basket.

The same product page also covers a smaller size. Keep the model and size selection attached to each figure you quote.

Sources for this section: S12 S13

The separate High Fry page

Chefman lists RJ38-8T-BLACK separately, with a matte-black finish, four functions and a Hi-Fry button described as raising the temperature to 450 °F for the final two minutes.

This establishes a documented difference between manufacturer models. It does not establish which one is behind an unconfirmed retailer listing.

Sources for this section: S26

Why the repository has not chosen a match

The dated Amazon listing observation combines a stainless description with Hi-Fry wording. The manufacturer pages do not explain that combination or provide an official ASIN-to-model confirmation here.

The earlier article ranked possible explanations and said the title described both machines. Those were inferences, not a verified identity. They have been removed.

Sources for this section: S29 S12 S26

What would settle the buying question

Confirm the selected listing’s model with the seller or manufacturer and match the full code to the relevant instructions. A listing field, the manual supplied and the unit’s label should agree. If the requested feature is important and the evidence remains inconsistent, ask before purchasing rather than assuming that the title is right.
