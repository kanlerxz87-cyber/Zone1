Model watch

The 8-quart Chefman is sold as two different machines — and the listing describes both

One SKU tops out at 400°F. Another reaches 450°F for the final two minutes. The supplied Amazon link's title names the feature of one and the finish of the other.

Explainer· 5 min read· sources read 2026-11-14· Documentation-based — no hands-on testing Cluster: What do the numbers actually mean?
No hands-on testing, and no Amazon numbers

Nobody here cooked with these appliances. Every specification below is quoted from the manufacturer's own product page or owner's manual and links to it. No Amazon price, star rating or review count appears, because Amazon's Program Policies only permit those when Amazon serves the link or via Creators/PA API S2.

Products covered
  • Air Fryer 8 Qt — TurboFry 4-in-1 (stainless steel) — Kitchen appliances → Air fryers

Two official pages, two temperature ceilings

Chefman's own store publishes a stainless 8-quart unit with a documented temperature range of 200–400 °F and four cooking presets.

On a different product page, Chefman publishes a matte-black 8-quart unit, model RJ38-8T-BLACK, with a 'Hi-Fry' button that raises the temperature to 450°F for the final two minutes and lists exactly four functions: air fry, bake, dehydrate and frozen.

Sources for this section: S12 S26

Why this is not a contradiction — it is a catalogue

Two model numbers two different products. What makes it confusing is the supplied Amazon listing: its title reads 'Chefman Air Fryer 8 Qt | TurboFry 4-in-1 with 450°F Hi-Fry Option for Crispier Results | Bake, Dehydrate, Frozen | XL Nonstick Dishwasher-Safe Basket | Stainless Steel'.

The 450°F Hi-Fry and the exact function list match the black RJ38-8T-BLACK page. The finish matches the stainless page. A title can be edited independently of a SKU, and Amazon does not publish the model number in a place a search engine reliably reads, so this site does not choose between them.

Sources for this section: S29 S26 S12

What to do with this if you are buying

Open the listing and read the model number in the Product information section. If it says RJ38-8T-BLACK, expect the 450°F Hi-Fry button and four functions. If it says RJ38-SQSS-8T, expect a 400°F ceiling and four presets.

That single line resolves a question this repository has carried as an open flag through seven verification passes, because it is the one fact that only a rendered listing page shows.
