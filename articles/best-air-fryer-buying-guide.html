Air Fryer Buying Guide 2026: Basket, Dual-Basket or French-Door Oven?

Three air fryers, three form factors, and one honest question: which shape actually fits your kitchen? This guide compares only what the manufacturers publish — capacity, temperature, functions, footprint and warranty — and links each specification back to its official product page.

The three shapes, and what each one is for

Basket fryers are single-drawer machines. The 8 Qt Chefman TurboFry in this programme is one: one 8 Qt / 7.57 L basket, four functions (air fry, bake, dehydrate, frozen) and a 450 °F Hi-Fry button for the final two minutes of cooking S8. It is designed to be narrow on the counter and feeds four to six.

Dual-basket fryers split that drawer in two. The 6 Qt Chefman here is two independent 3 Qt baskets that can run on their own settings, with Sync Finish so both finish at the same moment S7. The practical difference is that a main and a side stop competing for the same basket.

Oven-style air fryers stop being fryers and become small convection ovens. The Emeril Lagasse French Door AirFryer 360 is 26 Qt / 24.6 L with French doors, a rotisserie and a ten-function list, in a 16.9" D x 15.8" W x 14.8" H box S9. It can hold a 12-inch pizza, which no basket fryer in this set can.

How to choose

  1. Constraint is "two foods at once on different settings"? Dual-basket. 6 Qt total across two 3 Qt baskets.
  2. Constraint is batch size in one basket? 8 Qt single basket, with the 450 °F finish.
  3. Constraint is whole meals, baking or rotisserie? The 26 Qt oven format — and accept the counter depth it needs.
