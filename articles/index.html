How to read these articles

A highlighted number is a value published in this site's verified dataset and is a quotation from a manufacturer page or owner's manual. An ordinary number in prose is a date or a page reference. Anything marked judgement or written as a decision rule is the site's opinion, not a sourced fact. No product in this set was tested by anyone here, so no article claims a cooking result, a measured time or a hands-on comparison.
