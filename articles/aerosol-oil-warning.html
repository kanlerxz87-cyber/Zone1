Care, documented

The aerosol-oil warning appears in both Chefman manuals

The two manuals use different wording, but both caution against aerosol spray on nonstick parts. An earlier article incorrectly said the 8-quart manual had no rule.

The dual-window wording

The RJ38-SQPF-3TDB-2W manual cautions against spraying the baskets or racks with cooking oil from an aerosol can because harsh components can chip the coating. It says they may be oiled or sprayed from a non-aerosol mister.

This is the manufacturer’s care instruction, not a coating-durability test by this publication.

Sources for this section: S18

The 8-quart wording is explicit too

The RJ38-SQSS-8T manual states: “Never use aerosol spray on the air fryer’s nonstick parts as it can cause the coating to chip.” The next sentence allows oil from a mister.

That warning is in the operating instructions, not only the cleaning section. The shorter care row in this site’s dataset did not prove that the manual lacked the instruction.

Sources for this section: S13

The oil instructions do not stop there

Both manuals also say not to fill the baskets with oil and describe lightly oiling food separately where appropriate. Their cooking tips discuss food-specific use. It would be inaccurate to say that the only oil instruction is a spray-can warning, or that no quantities are ever suggested.

Sources for this section: S13 S18

Use the instructions for the exact model

Check the manual supplied with your unit, including cooling, washing and non-submersion instructions. Do not transfer a permission or prohibition to a different appliance just because the coating looks similar. Following a care instruction is not a promise of a particular service life.
