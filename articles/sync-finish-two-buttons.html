Sync Finish and Sync Baskets: two buttons, two promises, one that breaks

The Chefman 6 Qt manual defines both sync modes in plain sentences — including the exact mid-cook actions that end the finish-time promise.

Feature explainer · 5 min read · sources read 2026-11-14 · no first-hand testing

No hands-on testing, and no Amazon numbers

Nobody here cooked with these appliances. Every specification below is quoted from the manufacturer's own product page or owner's manual and links to it.   • TurboFry Touch 6 Qt. Dual-Basket / Dual-Window Air Fryer — Kitchen appliances → Air fryers

Two features that sound like one

The published spec row packs both into one line: Yes — delays the start of the shorter cook so both baskets finish together; a separate Sync Baskets function copies time and temperature to the second basket The distinction matters when you are planning two dishes.

The manual's definitions, verbatim: Sync Baskets is 'a shortcut for setting both baskets to cook simultaneously with the same time and temperature'. Sync Finish lets you 'program two foods with different cooking times and/or temperatures to finish cooking at the same time', by delaying the start of the basket with the shorter cook time — which then displays HOLD while it waits.

Sources for this section: S10 S18

The ways the sync ends

The manual is specific about breakage: 'If either timer is adjusted while both sides are actively cooking (including if you press the +2 MIN or HI-FRY button), then the sync will break, and the SYNC FINISH LED will turn off (but both baskets will continue cooking).' In Sync Baskets mode the rule is narrower: adjusting one basket's time or temperature makes the other follow, but pressing HI-FRY or +2 MIN breaks the sync.

So the browning button and the finish-time promise are mutually exclusive mid-cook. Pressing HI-FRY raises the active basket to 450 °F / 235 °C, held for 2 minutes, for extra browning — and ends the very coordination it was presumably meant to improve. Judgement: decide before you press, not during.

One more mechanical detail: removing a basket pauses both sides. That is a pause, not a break — the sync resumes when the basket goes back in.

Sources for this section: S18

What it is for, in honest numbers

The unit is 6 Qt / 5.67 L split into two 3-quart baskets, each independently programmed, over a 200–450 °F / 95–235 °C range. The realistic use of Sync Finish is a main dish and a side that land at the table together; Sync Baskets covers the simpler case where both dishes want the same settings.

What the documents never claim: equal crispness across baskets, identical doneness, or the same cook time for different foods. No number for any of those exists on the brand page or in the manual, and no unit was tested here. Decision rule: same settings, one button — Sync Baskets; different settings, one finish time — Sync Finish; mid-cook adjustments — expect the sync to end and watch both baskets yourself.
