Sync Finish: what it coordinates, and what breaks the sync

The manual describes a scheduling feature, not a promise that two different foods will be safely cooked at the same instant.

Sync Finish delays the shorter cook

For RJ38-SQPF-3TDB-2W, the manual says to set each basket, select Sync Finish and start. The longer cook begins first; the other basket displays HOLD until its delayed start.

Sync Baskets is different: it copies the same time and temperature to both baskets. It is a shortcut for matching settings, not a second name for staggered starts.

Basket removal pauses both in the sync modes

The sync instructions say that removing one basket pauses both sides until the basket is returned. That behaviour matters when checking or turning food. The same passage describes the shake reminder; it does not remove the cook’s responsibility to inspect the food.

Sources for this section: S18

More than two buttons can break Sync Finish

Hi-Fry and +2 MIN break sync. The Sync Finish steps also say that adjusting either timer while both sides are actively cooking breaks sync; the two baskets continue cooking, but the shared schedule no longer applies.

While one basket is still waiting, the finish remains synced only under the manual’s stated condition: the shorter time must not be increased above the longer one. Read the complete sequence rather than assuming all mid-cook changes preserve the schedule.

Sources for this section: S18

A finish time is not a food-safety result

The manual instructs the cook to check both baskets for doneness. Different quantities and foods may need more time even when the timers end together.

Editorial judgement: use Sync Finish when coordinating separate settings is useful, but do not treat its name as a replacement for checking food. This publication has not tested the timing or cooking result.
