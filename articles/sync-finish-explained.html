What does Sync Finish actually synchronise?

On the documented Chefman dual-window model, the feature coordinates the end of two cook cycles. That is a timing function—not a doneness test.

By Zone1 Kitchen · AI-assisted, document-based writing· 3 min read· Sources checked 2026-11-19· Updated 19 Nov 2026 Documentation-based — no hands-on testing Cluster: Which machine should I buy?

No appliance was tested for this guide. Specifications are attributed to their documents; editorial guidance is not a cooking result. How we research · first-publication timestamp is not established.

Sync Finish and Sync Baskets do different jobs

The manual describes setting each basket’s time and temperature, then using Sync Finish to delay the shorter cook so the two cycles end together.

Sync Baskets copies settings instead. Decide whether you want different settings with coordinated timing or the same settings on both sides; the names are not interchangeable.

Read what changes the schedule

The operating instructions explain the modes and how controls affect them. In particular, using Hi-Fry changes the synchronisation; its 450 °F operation is a two-minute boost, not an unlimited full-cycle promise.

Use the full current instructions rather than treating the button name as a guarantee that every later adjustment stays synced. This guide is a feature explanation, not a replacement operating manual.

Sources for this section: S18

What it cannot tell you

Two cycles ending together does not prove that two different foods are cooked through or safe to serve. This publication has not cooked a meal in the appliance or assessed a recipe.

The function belongs to the documented dual-window model. Do not transfer it to the single-basket 8 Qt model or assume that nominal capacity increases because cooking is coordinated.
