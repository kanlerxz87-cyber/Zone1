What to write next, and how it is allowed to be promoted

Fourteen topics for this product set — 11 already published and 3 not yet written — and five distribution channels, two of which Amazon's policies close off entirely. The topics are chosen for one reason: each one is answerable from an official manufacturer page or an owner's manual, so none of them requires a claim this site cannot support.

Read this before treating anything below as a fact.
  • Everything on this page is a proposal or a judgement unless it is explicitly marked as sourced. No topic here has been written, and none of them is a claim about a product.
  • There is no keyword-volume data anywhere in this repository, and none was invented for this page. Any statement about what people search for is labelled as judgement and is an argument from the product's own documentation, not a measurement. Publishing estimated search volumes without a measurement tool would be the exact failure this project exists to avoid.
  • Every article proposed here is buildable from official manufacturer pages and owner's manuals only. None of them requires a hands-on test, and none may claim one: the FTC warns that you cannot talk about your experience with a product you have not tried (S8).
  • Each row names the official source the finished article would have to cite. If a row's sources cannot support its headline, the headline has to change - not the citation.

TP-04 · Transactional · not yet written

The counter-space test: measure before you buy

The angle. All three exterior dimensions are published from official sources, and they are wildly different shapes: a 13.4 x 11.8 x 10.6 in dual-basket box, a 15.4 x 12.6 x 13.5 in single basket, and a 16.9 x 15.8 x 14.8 in oven. Height under a cabinet is the one that actually fails.

Why it could earn traffic. Judgement: 'will it fit under my cabinet' is a pre-purchase question with high intent and almost no good answers, and this site already holds three sourced dimension sets. It is the cheapest article here to write well.

Must cite. Chefman 6 Qt product page — dimensions ↗ Chefman 8 Qt product page — 8 Qt dimensions ↗ Emeril product page — 16.9D x 15.8W x 14.8H ↗
