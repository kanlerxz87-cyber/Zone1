The rule. Buy the format, not the brand: two baskets if you cook two different things at once, a French-door oven if you also want to replace a toaster oven, one big basket if you are cooking for four to six people from a single batch. Everything else on this page is a consequence of that rule.

  • A sale is only attributed if the click came through a correctly tagged Special Link. The three supplied links show no tag in their landing URL, so as supplied they are evidence of a destination, not of an attribution path.
  • The category that applies to a given ASIN is not published publicly. 4.50% vs 4.00% is a $0.60 difference on a $120 item; it is not worth guessing, and it is not worth blocking the site over.

Why this page is not a top-ten list

Because "best" needs evidence this site does not have

A ranking implies comparison testing. The FTC's own guidance says you cannot talk about experience with a product you have not tried, and Amazon's policies restrict republishing customer reviews and star ratings. So this page ranks jobs, not products, and shows the specification that supports each match.

Because the reader can check every line

Cookies & privacy

This site sets no cookies itself, runs no analytics, and has no comment forms or accounts. The outbound-click counter on these pages counts clicks in the browser's own memory for the current visit and sends nothing anywhere, because no endpoint is configured yet. Following an Amazon link takes you to Amazon, whose own privacy notice and cookie practices apply from that point. No data you enter here is collected, because there is nowhere to enter it.

Amazon's Program Policies §3(e) require an accurate statement of cookie and third-party technology use on Associate sites. This notice satisfies that requirement for the current build; it updates automatically the moment an analytics endpoint is configured.
