FTC requirements (U.S.)

#RuleCompliant practice on this projectSource
FT1 Disclose any financial, employment, personal, or family relationship with a brand — including free or discounted products — whenever you endorse. Affiliate disclosure bar on every page; repeat disclosure at the top of content pages. FTC — Disclosures 101
FT2 Placement: “hard to miss”, with the endorsement itself; not only on an About page, at the end, or behind “MORE”. Disclosure directly above product copy on the Content page. FTC — Disclosures 101
FT3 Use simple, clear language (“advertisement”, “ad”, “sponsored”); avoid vague terms like “collab”, “ambassador”. Plain-language “As an Amazon Associate…” statement plus explicit commission language near recommendations. FTC — Disclosures 101
FT4 Video/stream disclosures must be in the medium itself (audio + video; repeated in live streams). Applies when video content is added — recorded as a launch checklist item. FTC — Disclosures 101
FT5 “You can't talk about your experience with a product you haven't tried”; no unsubstantiated claims. Copy describes official listing information only; explicitly states no hands-on testing is claimed. FTC — Disclosures 101

Pre-publish compliance checklist

  • ☐ Disclosure statement present and clearly visible (OA §5).
  • ☐ Every product claim traceable to the verified-attributes store (PR §2(b): no misleading claims).
  • ☐ No prices/ratings unless PA-API provenance + timestamp + disclaimer (PR §2(b), §6(t), IP License §2(i)).
  • ☐ All monetized links are tagged Special Links; destinations are amazon.com and clear (PR §2(v)(w)).
  • ☐ No price tracking/alerts (PR §2(y)); no incentives for clicks (PR §2(g)).
  • ☐ Agents used in publishing self-identify and do not circumvent site protections (IP License §4).
