Click & conversion tracking

What may be measured, how attribution works (verified from the Commission Income Statement), KPI formulas, and the tracking that is explicitly prohibited. No performance metrics are shown yet — none exist until tagged links are live, and inventing them would violate this project's data rules.

Attribution model (verified)

EventRule (official)ValueSource
Session start “a customer clicks through a Special Link on your Site to an Amazon Site”. Click CIS §1
Session end First of: 24 hours elapse, customer places an order (non-digital), or customer clicks another Associate's Special Link. 24 h CIS §1
Cart credit Items added to cart in-session and ordered ≤ 89 days after the initial click-through qualify. 89 days CIS §1
Ship & pay condition Within 180 days of purchase the product must be shipped/streamed/downloaded and paid for. 180 days CIS §1
Disqualifiers Cancellations/returns/refunds; improperly formatted links; Prohibited Paid Search Placements; Redirecting Links; self/related-party purchases (§2(u)); artificial clicks (§2(s)). — CIS §2
Reporting source of truth Amazon's Associates Central reports summarize commission income; paid ~60 days after month end. Associates Central CIS §6

Optimization loop

  • Measure: per-page click-through via first-party analytics; commissions via Associates Central reports.
  • Compare: EPC and conversion by article (S1–S7) and by product.
  • Improve: rewrite low-CTR blocks, re-order comparison tables, expand winning content clusters.
  • Guardrails: never incentivize clicks (§2(g)), never fabricate engagement (§2(s)), keep disclosures adjacent to links.
