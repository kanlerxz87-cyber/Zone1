Read this before you use any number on this site

No Amazon price, star rating or review count appears anywhere on this site. That is deliberate. Amazon's Program Policies §2(b) allow price and availability to be shown only when Amazon serves the link or when the data comes from Creators/PA API, and §3(t) restricts customer reviews and star ratings to API-served content S2. Manufacturer list prices are shown instead, in their own column, with their own source links.
