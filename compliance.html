Policy text and implementation boundaries

This is a cited policy register, not Amazon approval or legal certification. Implementation notes were reconciled on 19 November; quotes retain their recorded source dates. Only the named sections in the scoped report were freshly re-read.

19 requirements that govern this site, each with the source text, a link to the official document, and a bounded statement of what this repository does about it. Where a rule is inconvenient, the rule wins and the feature is dropped. 1 of them is gated rather than met — named in the register below with the step that would clear it. A register that showed every row green while a revenue path was switched off would be the kind of page this project exists not to publish.

The rule that governs whether a click can be paid on

Amazon requires the Associates tag to appear as a parameter in the URL of each link, and pays only where a purchase follows a click through one of those Special Links. This site currently emits untagged product links, so it cannot be attributed a sale. That is a deliberate, reversible gate, not an editorial position: see C19 below and F76. S2

The three rules that shape everything else

Prices may only be shown when Amazon serves the link or via the official API. Reviews and star ratings may only be shown when obtained through that API. Program Content may not be aggregated, analysed, repurposed or used to train a model. Those three are why this site has no Amazon prices, no Amazon ratings, and no Amazon scraping pipeline — even though all three would have been easier to build. S2

Affiliate disclosure — exact wording
 The site must clearly and prominently state the Associate identification statement.
“You must clearly and prominently state the following, or any substantially similar statement previously allowed under this Agreement, on your Site or any other location where Amazon may authorize your display or other use of Program Content: ‘As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases.’”Operating Agreement §5

Link-level disclosure must be clear and conspicuous
 Beyond the site-wide statement, each affiliate link needs a clear, nearby, FTC-compliant disclosure.
“To comply with the Federal Trade Commission (FTC) regulations, your link-level disclosure must be: 1. Clear. Disclosure placement rules (FTC)
 Disclosures must be hard to miss, in the same medium as the endorsement, and must not be buried or mixed into hashtags.
“Make sure people will see and understand the disclosure. Place it so it's hard to miss. The disclosure should be placed with the endorsement message itself. Disclosures are likely to be missed if they appear only on an ABOUT ME or profile page, at the end of posts or videos, or anywhere that requires a person to click MORE. Material connection (FTC rule text)
 A financial relationship with the seller must be disclosed.
When there exists a connection between the endorser and the seller of the advertised product that might materially affect the weight or credibility of the endorsement, and that connection is not reasonably expected by the audience, such connection must be disclosed clearly and conspicuously.16 CFR Part 255, §255.5(a)
 S9 The potential financial relationship is disclosed in the strip, beside main product CTAs and in the footer. No credited click, order or earnings report is claimed.

 Prices and availability may only be displayed if Amazon serves them or they are obtained through Creators API/PA API.

Customer reviews and star ratings
 Amazon reviews/star ratings may only be displayed when obtained through Creators API or PA API.
“You will not display or otherwise use any of our customer reviews or star ratings, in part or in whole, on your Site unless you have obtained a link to that customer review or star rating through Creators API or PA API and you comply with the requirements for Creators API and PA API described in the License.”Program Policies — Participation Requirements §6(t) ("Content on your Site"; live page read 2026-11-14 — see flag F27)

No price tracking or price alerts
 Sites must not offer price tracking or price alerting unless Amazon agrees otherwise.
“Unless otherwise agreed by Amazon, your Site must not have price tracking and/or price alerting functionality.”Program Policies — Participation Requirements §6(y) ("Content on your Site"; live page read 2026-11-14 — see flag F27)
 S2 No price-history, alert, deal-timer or lowest-ever component ships. No revenue scenario or conversion score is used to justify design. Any future authorised exception requires a separate current permission/terms review.
C8
Prohibited paid search placement
 No bidding on Amazon or Kindle trademarks, or any Amazon Mark, in paid search.
“…any Product purchased by a customer who is referred to an Amazon Site through any advertisement that you purchased, including through participation in bidding or auctions on keywords, search terms, or other identifiers that include the word ‘amazon’, or ‘kindle’, or any other Amazon Mark…” (disqualified purchases)Commission Income Statement §2(c)
 S2 The paid-search restriction is recorded in the blueprint. No paid campaign, live bidding workflow or enforced account negative-keyword configuration is deployed or claimed.
C9
No API content aggregation, analysis or model training
 Creators API / PA API / Data Feeds content may not be aggregated, analysed, repurposed, or used to train or fine-tune ML models.
“You will not, without our express prior written approval, access or use Creators API, PA API or Data Feeds for the purpose of aggregating, analyzing, extracting, or repurposing any Product Advertising Content or in connection with any software or other application intended for use by persons or entities that offer products on an Amazon Site, or in the direct training or fine-tuning of a machine learning and/or foundational model.”Program Policies — IP License §2(e)
 S2 No authorised API/content-analysis feed or running trend scorer is configured. Future access and permitted-use review are separate gates; the static builder does not grant rights to analyse or train on Program Content.
C10
Original content requirement
 Sites must carry robust original content — roughly 10+ posts, recent, publicly available.
“All Sites must have robust original content (even when advertising is removed) – a good rule of thumb is at least 10 posts. They must be publicly available…” and “Content on your website must be recent (generally within the last 60 days). You must own your website.”Application Review Process
 S4 This build ships analysis, comparison and buying-guide pages written for this dataset, plus the platform blueprint — not a thin link list.
C11
Three qualifying sales in 180 days
 Applications are reviewed after three qualifying sales within the first 180 days; personal orders do not count.
“After you sign up, our Associates team will check your application once you’ve driven qualified sales (we require at least three within the first 180 days). Please note that personal orders do not qualify.”Application Review Process
 S4 Public criteria remain a dated policy record, not a claim about this account’s approval, qualifying sales or review-window start. PUB-1 did not freshly re-read the application criteria page or access the account.
C12
No incentive for using links; no self-purchase
 No rewards/loyalty schemes driving clicks; no buying through your own links or asking friends/family to.
“You will not offer any person or entity any consideration, reward, or incentive (including any money, rebate, discount, points, donation to charity or other organization, or other benefit) for using Special Links…” / “You will not directly or indirectly purchase any Product(s)… through Special Links…”Participation Requirements §6(g) and §6(u) ("Content on your Site"; live page read 2026-11-14 — see flag F27)
 S2 No incentives, no contests, no employee purchase flows. Guardrail G4.
C13
No framing, no cloaking, no redirecting links
 Amazon pages may not be framed; Special Links may not be cloaked; redirecting links that skip user action are prohibited.
“You will not frame an Amazon Site, or any part of it, within your Site…” / “You will not cloak, hide, spoof, or otherwise obscure the URL of your Site containing Special Links (including by use of Redirecting Links)…”Participation Requirements §6(n) and §6(v) ("Content on your Site"; live page read 2026-11-14 — see flag F27)
 S2 No iframes. Outbound links point straight at the Amazon URL; no interstitial or meta-refresh forwarding is generated by the site.
C14
Tracking IDs and sub-tags
 Reporting IDs are permitted, but they may not be tied to an individual end user.
“…we may issue you additional ‘sub-tag’ Associate IDs that permit you to monitor and optimize the performance of your Special Links… Under no circumstances may you associate any sub-tag, other ID or reporting provided in connection with the Associates Program with a specific end user of your Site (e.g., you may not dynamically assign sub-tags to users as they arrive on your Site for purposes of monitoring such users’ behavior).”Participation Requirements §2(b)
 S2 shopnow047c-20 is selected but unvalidated and unapplied. No channel/page IDs have been created or assigned by this build. Any later approved ID plan must not identify individual visitors.
C15
Trademark use
 Amazon Marks may be used only to advertise availability of products on an Amazon Site, alongside a corresponding Special Link, unaltered, and never in offline material.
“YOU ARE ALLOWED TO USE THE AMAZON MARKS ONLY BY DISPLAY ON YOUR SITE WITH THE PURPOSE OF ADVERTISING AVAILABILITY OF PRODUCTS ON AN AMAZON SITE, WITH A CORRESPONDING SPECIAL LINK TO THAT SITE.”Trademark Guidelines §1
 S2 No Amazon logo is copied into the canonical publication. Plain-text Amazon labels make outbound destinations clear; this is not evidence that an enrolled Special Link or account has been validated.
C16
Honest claims about products
 No inaccurate, overbroad, deceptive or misleading product/policy/price claims; no claiming an experience you have not had.
“You must not make inaccurate, overbroad, deceptive or otherwise misleading claims about any Product, an Amazon Site, or any of our policies, promotions, or prices.” / FTC: “You can't talk about your experience with a product you haven't tried.”Participation Requirements §2(b); Disclosures 101
 S2 S8 AI-assisted document research and no hands-on testing are disclosed. Revised guide claims cite named official sections; uncertain values and conflicts remain explicit. Link coverage alone is not proof that arbitrary prose is true.
C17
Agents must identify themselves, must not obfuscate, must not defeat bot checks
 If software or a service takes autonomous or semi-autonomous action on the Program Content, it is an “Agent”: it must identify itself in every HTTP/HTTPS request, must not conceal that it is an agent, must not complete or circumvent CAPTCHAs, and must answer truthfully when asked whether the interactions are human.
“The terms in this section (“Agent Terms”) apply if you use, allow, enable, or cause the deployment of an Agent to access, use, or interact with the Program Content. For the purposes of these Agent Terms, “Agent” means any software or service that takes autonomous or semi-autonomous action on behalf of, or at the instruction of, any person or entity.” … “In all HTTP/HTTPS requests, identify that the request is from an Agent and disclose the name of the Agent by including the following in the request’s user agent string: “Agent/[agent name]” (e.g., Agent/AmazonAgent)” … “Not conceal or obfuscate that any access, use, or interactions are from an Agent, such as by (a) mimicking the speed or pattern of human keystrokes, page navigation, or other interactions or (b) completing or circumventing CAPTCHAs or other measures intended to distinguish computers from humans. Respond truthfully to any question or prompt seeking to determine if interactions are coming from a human or a computer.”Program Policies — Associates Program IP License §4 (Agents). Read live 2026-11-16 by arena/9fccce0c-zone1; the whole of §4 was not read end to end in that pass (flag F62).
 S2 No unattended network research agent is deployed by this static build. The Agent Terms and identity/access requirements are recorded as future research gates. This is not a legal finding that no Agent Term can apply to an external tool or to every future use.
C18
No Special Links or Program Content used to develop machine-learning models without approval
 An Associate may not use Special Links or Program Content — or allow a third party to do so — to develop machine-learning models or related technology, without Amazon's express prior written approval.
“You will not, without express prior written approval, use, or allow any third party to use, any Special Links or Program Content to develop machine learning models or related technology.”Program Policies — Participation Requirements §5 (Distribution of Special Links Through Software and Devices). Read live 2026-11-16 by arena/9fccce0c-zone1.
 S2 This repository implements no model-training/fine-tuning pipeline. Future Special Link or Program Content use requires current permission/terms review; no API credentials, written approval or legal entitlement are invented.
C19
The Associates tag must be a URL parameter on every Special Link
 A tracked outbound link is not merely a product URL. Amazon requires the Associates ID ("tag") to appear as a parameter in the URL of each link placed on the site, requires Special Links to be accessed directly from the site, forbids encouraging visitors to bookmark them, and makes the link the unit at which a Qualifying Purchase is created: a purchase can only be credited if it follows a click through a Special Link.
“All Special Links must be accessed directly from your Site. For example, you must include your Associates ID or ‘tag’ (appearing as XXXXX-##, or such other format as we may designate) as a parameter in the URL of each link you place on your Site to an Amazon Site.” — and “We will pay Standard Commission Income … in connection with ‘Qualifying Purchases’, which … occur when: (a) a customer clicks through a Special Link on your Site to an Amazon Site”.Program Policies — Participation Requirements §2(b); Commission Income Statement §1
 S2 GatedNOT SATISFIED ON THE PUBLISHED BUILD, BY DESIGN. data/config.json keeps associate_tag empty, so every outbound product anchor the generator emits is an untagged product URL: it can send a reader to the right listing but cannot create an attributed Qualifying Purchase. That is the correct state until the account-holder checks in F76 pass, because the repository must not invent a tag or claim attribution it has not observed. The consequence is stated plainly rather than buried: while the tag is blank the affiliate revenue path is not operating, regardless of how much traffic the pages earn. Enabling it is a configuration change, not a rewrite — the generator already routes every product anchor through one builder and qualifies every one sponsored. Gates: build.py refuses to write a tag that has not been confirmed in Associates Central or whose per-product enrolled test is unrecorded; verify.py re-checks that no unverified tag reached the output.

What a visitor should take from this page

If you are the buyer

The exact Amazon Associate disclosure appears at the top of every page. Product buttons also state that the supplied link's model identity and tracking tag are unconfirmed, followed by a link-level explanation. The disclosure is generated into the layout and cannot be removed by a content update.

If you are the operator

This is the audit surface. When Amazon changes a rule, the page changes here first, then the build enforces it. The flagged page is the backlog of facts that still need a human decision before any copy can assert them.

Privacy, local drafts & hosting

This site sets no cookies itself, runs no analytics, and sends no measurement events. The self-hosted collector is implemented and tested, but is not deployed on this GitHub Pages site. Browser collection is disabled.

GitHub Pages separately logs visitor IP addresses for security. This publisher does not control those host logs. GitHub’s hosting notice. Following an Amazon link takes you to Amazon, where its own privacy and cookie practices apply. Workspace decision drafts, if used, stay in your browser’s local storage until cleared; they are not sent to a server.
