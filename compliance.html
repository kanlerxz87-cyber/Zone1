Compliance, quoted rather than summarised

16 requirements that govern this site, each with the source text, a link to the official document, and a plain statement of how the build satisfies it. Where a rule is inconvenient, the rule wins and the feature is dropped.

The three rules that shape everything else

Prices may only be shown when Amazon serves the link or via the official API. Reviews and star ratings may only be shown when obtained through that API. Program Content may not be aggregated, analysed, repurposed or used to train a model. Those three are why this site has no Amazon prices, no Amazon ratings, and no Amazon scraping pipeline — even though all three would have been easier to build. S2

Requirement register

Program and legal requirements mapped to implementation
RequirementWhat the rule saysSourceHow this build complies
C1
Affiliate disclosure — exact wording
 The site must clearly and prominently state the Associate identification statement.
“You must clearly and prominently state the following, or any substantially similar statement previously allowed under this Agreement, on your Site or any other location where Amazon may authorize your display or other use of Program Content: ‘As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases.’”Operating Agreement §5
 S1 The statement appears in the persistent header disclosure bar and in the footer of every page generated by build.py.
C2
Link-level disclosure must be clear and conspicuous
 Beyond the site-wide statement, each affiliate link needs a clear, nearby, FTC-compliant disclosure.
“To comply with the Federal Trade Commission (FTC) regulations, your link-level disclosure must be: 1. Clear. A clear disclosure could be as simple as ‘(paid link)’, ‘#ad’, or ‘#CommissionsEarned’. 2. Conspicuous. It should be placed near any affiliate link or product review in a location that customers will notice easily. They shouldn't have to hunt for it.”Associates help article (disclosure guidance)
 S5 Every outbound Amazon button carries a visible “Paid link” tag, and a disclosure note sits directly beneath each CTA.
C3
Disclosure placement rules (FTC)
 Disclosures must be hard to miss, in the same medium as the endorsement, and must not be buried or mixed into hashtags.
“Make sure people will see and understand the disclosure. Place it so it's hard to miss. The disclosure should be placed with the endorsement message itself. Disclosures are likely to be missed if they appear only on an ABOUT ME or profile page, at the end of posts or videos, or anywhere that requires a person to click MORE. Don't mix your disclosure into a group of hashtags or links.”Disclosures 101 — How to Disclose
 S8 Disclosure bar is above the fold on every page; per-link badges; no disclosure-only-in-footer pattern.
C4
Material connection (FTC rule text)
 A financial relationship with the seller must be disclosed.
16 CFR §255.5 requires disclosure of material connections between an endorser and the seller that would materially affect the weight or credibility of the endorsement.16 CFR Part 255, §255.5
 S9 Commission-earning relationship disclosed in the header, on each CTA and on the compliance page.
C5
Prices and availability
 Prices and availability may only be displayed if Amazon serves them or they are obtained through Creators API/PA API.
“Because prices for and availability of Products that you have listed on your Site may change, your Site may only show prices and availability if: (a) we serve the link in which that price and availability data are displayed, or (b) you obtain Product pricing and availability data via Creators API or PA API and you comply with the requirements…”Program Policies — Participation Requirements §2(b)
 S2 No Amazon price or availability is shown anywhere. Manufacturer list prices are shown separately and explicitly labelled as brand-site prices with their own source link.
C6
Customer reviews and star ratings
 Amazon reviews/star ratings may only be displayed when obtained through Creators API or PA API.
“You will not display or otherwise use any of our customer reviews or star ratings, in part or in whole, on your Site unless you have obtained a link to that customer review or star rating through Creators API or PA API and you comply with the requirements for Creators API and PA API described in the License.”Program Policies — Participation Requirements §3(t)
 S2 Amazon stars/review counts blank and flagged. Brand-hosted ratings are shown only in a clearly separate column, attributed to the brand's own site.
C7
No price tracking or price alerts
 Sites must not offer price tracking or price alerting unless Amazon agrees otherwise.
“Unless otherwise agreed by Amazon, your Site must not have price tracking and/or price alerting functionality.”Program Policies — Participation Requirements §3(y)
 S2 The platform blueprint explicitly rules out price-history charts and alerts; ‘pricing analysis’ is limited to brand-published list prices and unit economics computed from them.
C8
Prohibited paid search placement
 No bidding on Amazon or Kindle trademarks, or any Amazon Mark, in paid search.
“…any Product purchased by a customer who is referred to an Amazon Site through any advertisement that you purchased, including through participation in bidding or auctions on keywords, search terms, or other identifiers that include the word ‘amazon’, or ‘kindle’, or any other Amazon Mark…” (disqualified purchases)Commission Income Statement §2(c)
 S2 Guardrail G3 in the platform blueprint: a negative-keyword list containing Amazon Marks is enforced in the paid-acquisition workflow.
C9
No API content aggregation, analysis or model training
 Creators API / PA API / Data Feeds content may not be aggregated, analysed, repurposed, or used to train or fine-tune ML models.
“You will not, without our express prior written approval, access or use Creators API, PA API or Data Feeds for the purpose of aggregating, analyzing, extracting, or repurposing any Product Advertising Content or in connection with any software or other application intended for use by persons or entities that offer products on an Amazon Site, or in the direct training or fine-tuning of a machine learning and/or foundational model.”Program Policies — IP License §2(e)
 S2 Architectural constraint: any AI research/trend scoring runs only on the operator's own first-party data (clicks, conversions, revenue) and public manufacturer data. Program Content is display-only, never a training or analytics input.
C10
Original content requirement
 Sites must carry robust original content — roughly 10+ posts, recent, publicly available.
“All Sites must have robust original content (even when advertising is removed) – a good rule of thumb is at least 10 posts. They must be publicly available…” and “Content on your website must be recent (generally within the last 60 days). You must own your website.”Application Review Process
 S4 This build ships analysis, comparison and buying-guide pages written for this dataset, plus the platform blueprint — not a thin link list.
C11
Three qualifying sales in 180 days
 Applications are reviewed after three qualifying sales within the first 180 days; personal orders do not count.
“After you sign up, our Associates team will check your application once you’ve driven qualified sales (we require at least three within the first 180 days). Please note that personal orders do not qualify.”Application Review Process
 S4 Launch sequencing in the blueprint: publish the buying guide + three product pages first, then apply, so the 180-day clock starts with live traffic.
C12
No incentive for using links; no self-purchase
 No rewards/loyalty schemes driving clicks; no buying through your own links or asking friends/family to.
“You will not offer any person or entity any consideration, reward, or incentive (including any money, rebate, discount, points, donation to charity or other organization, or other benefit) for using Special Links…” / “You will not directly or indirectly purchase any Product(s)… through Special Links…”Participation Requirements §3(g) and §3(u)
 S2 No incentives, no contests, no employee purchase flows. Guardrail G4.
C13
No framing, no cloaking, no redirecting links
 Amazon pages may not be framed; Special Links may not be cloaked; redirecting links that skip user action are prohibited.
“You will not frame an Amazon Site, or any part of it, within your Site…” / “You will not cloak, hide, spoof, or otherwise obscure the URL of your Site containing Special Links (including by use of Redirecting Links)…”Participation Requirements §3(n) and §3(v)
 S2 No iframes. Outbound links point straight at the Amazon URL; no interstitial or meta-refresh forwarding is generated by the site.
C14
Tracking IDs and sub-tags
 Reporting IDs are permitted, but they may not be tied to an individual end user.
“…we may issue you additional ‘sub-tag’ Associate IDs that permit you to monitor and optimize the performance of your Special Links… Under no circumstances may you associate any sub-tag, other ID or reporting provided in connection with the Associates Program with a specific end user of your Site (e.g., you may not dynamically assign sub-tags to users as they arrive on your Site for purposes of monitoring such users’ behavior).”Participation Requirements §2(b)
 S2 Sub-tags are assigned per channel/page, never per visitor. Documented as guardrail G5.
C15
Trademark use
 Amazon Marks may be used only to advertise availability of products on an Amazon Site, alongside a corresponding Special Link, unaltered, and never in offline material.
“YOU ARE ALLOWED TO USE THE AMAZON MARKS ONLY BY DISPLAY ON YOUR SITE WITH THE PURPOSE OF ADVERTISING AVAILABILITY OF PRODUCTS ON AN AMAZON SITE, WITH A CORRESPONDING SPECIAL LINK TO THAT SITE.”Trademark Guidelines §1
 S2 No Amazon logo is used in this build; Amazon is referred to in plain text next to real Special Links only.
C16
Honest claims about products
 No inaccurate, overbroad, deceptive or misleading product/policy/price claims; no claiming an experience you have not had.
“You must not make inaccurate, overbroad, deceptive or otherwise misleading claims about any Product, an Amazon Site, or any of our policies, promotions, or prices.” / FTC: “You can't talk about your experience with a product you haven't tried.”Participation Requirements §2(b); Disclosures 101
 S2 S8 No hands-on testing claims are made anywhere. Copy is explicitly framed as documentation-based analysis, and every factual line links to the manufacturer page or manual.

What a visitor should take from this page

If you are the buyer

The disclosure bar at the top of every page is the one Amazon requires word for word. The "Paid link" tag next to every outbound button is the FTC-style link-level disclosure. Neither can be switched off by a content update, because both are generated into the page layout.

If you are the operator

This is the audit surface. When Amazon changes a rule, the page changes here first, then the build enforces it. The flagged page is the backlog of facts that still need a human decision before any copy can assert them.

Disclosure

As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases.

Links marked Paid link are Amazon Associates links. If you buy through one, this site may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. Amazon prices and availability are never published here — check the live price on Amazon.

Every asserted value on this site links to the official page it came from. Values that could not be confirmed from an official source are left blank and listed on the Flagged for review page.

Cookies & privacy

This site sets no cookies itself, runs no analytics, and has no comment forms or accounts. Following an Amazon link takes you to Amazon, whose own privacy notice and cookie practices apply from that point. No data you enter here is collected, because there is nowhere to enter it.

Amazon's Program Policies §3(e) require an accurate statement of cookie and third-party technology use on Associate sites. This notice satisfies that requirement for the current build; it must be updated the moment any analytics, embeds or affiliate-network script is added.
