## Defects found and repaired

### F107 — the short-link source entry named three of the five supplied tokens

`S17` called itself "link.amazon short links supplied by the account holder" and enumerated
three tokens (B04G1MEwY, B02NbDxp3, B00hJ57DV). Five were supplied: B0c8moW59 and B0f3GKalj
arrived on 2026-11-17.

**What was not wrong.** This was first written up here as a mis-citation — two intent rows
resting on a source that excluded them. Re-reading the rows against the register showed that
was false and the claim was rewritten before merge. PI-04 cites **S36** and PI-05 cites **S37**;
both of those entries state the token each listing was reached through. No published
destination was unsupported, and nothing was mis-sourced.

**What was wrong** was the register's own scope sentence, which invites the next pass to cite
S17 for two links it does not describe. The repair:

1. **S17** now names the three tokens it covers and says it does not describe the other two.
2. **S52** registers `https://link.amazon/B0c8moW59` and **S53** registers
   `https://link.amazon/B0f3GKalj` as short-link sources in their own right, each recording only
   what its own token served on 2026-11-20, with the served `<title>` quoted.
3. PI-04 and PI-05 cite `[S36, S52]` and `[S37, S53]` — the token **alongside** the listing row,
   not instead of it.
4. Every one of the five rows gained a dated 2026-11-20 entry in `observation_history`, and the
   row-level `observed_on`, `observed_title` and `observed_redirect_target` were **not**
   overwritten: they remain the 2026-11-19 reading, and each new entry says so, including that
   the served `<title>` carries Amazon's site-name chrome.

Unchanged and still blank on all five rows: `intended_asin`, `official_listing_url`,
`confirmation_date`, `confirmation_source`, `associates_link_test_passed`. Resolved destinations
stay in their own `observed_*` field. Nothing here says a token is valid or invalid (Zone3
FLG-003), and a visible-or-absent `tag=` on a landing URL is not attribution evidence (S41).

### F108 — an indexable page nobody could reach

`picks.html` was listed in `sitemap.xml` and carried `index,follow`, but the internal
link graph showed **zero** inbound links from any other page. The only pages naming it
were two owner-workspace registers. It was also a near-copy of the front page's three
product cards.

Repair, in the generator rather than the HTML:

- The page now opens with four reader questions (separate settings, capacity, counter
  fit, cleaning), each routing to the guide that already answers it from the same
  official documents. No new factual claim was introduced; the extension-cord phrasing
  was deliberately softened because the Emeril manual contradicts itself on cords (F96).
- It is now reachable from the footer's "Find your fit" group on **every** page, from
  the home page's question strip, and from the comparison page.
- Re-counted after the rebuild: 61 inbound links.

Residual, left for the owner: the page still overlaps the home page's format section.
Whether it stays a standalone format finder or folds into the category page is a design
judgement, not a factual one.
