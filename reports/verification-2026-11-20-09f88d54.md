## Executive result

The existing canonical publication satisfies the repository-checkable parts of the brief: it presents a buyer-first kitchen publication, organizes the products and guides by category and question, keeps the owner/evidence surfaces in one navigation group and footer, publishes a sitemap and RSS feed, and provides sourced product pages, a comparison, a design/suggestions desk, decision questions, a requirements register, an executive summary, and a public Flagged for Review page.

The site is **not** a deployed autonomous Amazon research or revenue-optimization service. The current implementation is a static publication plus a tested, disabled first-party analytics reference collector and an automation blueprint. Five unresolved product-intent records and the selected tracking ID remain separate from observed destinations. The Associates account, intended listings, production analytics host, Search Console property, campaign results, and product-image permissions cannot be verified from repository access alone. No clicks, orders, trend, conversion, revenue, ranking, or popularity figures are claimed.

No product or policy values were edited by this pass. The local gates below were rerun after rebasing onto both commits; they validate the committed artifacts and contracts, not source semantics, current remote availability, or a legal opinion.
