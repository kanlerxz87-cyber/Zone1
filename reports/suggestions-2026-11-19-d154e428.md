## The honest position, stated plainly

The site is no longer an audit of itself. It leads with products, it discloses its
affiliate relationship on every page, and every outbound Amazon product link is marked
sponsored. Its sourcing discipline is real: values that cannot be confirmed are blank and
flagged rather than guessed.

What it still cannot do — and no amount of additional publishing will change this — is
**prove that anyone bought anything.** That is not a content problem. It is three
account-level decisions and one deployment.

## The four things worth your attention, in order

### 1. Confirm what each link is supposed to sell (DR-01, P0)

All five supplied links resolve. They reach five real product pages. What is missing is
your statement that those are the products you intend. Until you confirm it, every
product page must say "listing unconfirmed", and the four intent fields stay blank.

This is the highest-leverage ten minutes in the project. It removes the biggest caveat on
every buyer page without a single line of new writing.

- Evidence already held: the five observed ASINs, dated 2026-11-19.
- Official context: Amazon on why a tracking ID does not need to appear in the URL —
  <https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/help/node/topic/GF4RJEQNXSMR7SP3>

### 2. Validate and apply the tracking ID (DR-02, P0)

`shopnow047c-20` is recorded but unvalidated and unapplied. Amazon documents that
separate tracking IDs can be reported separately, which is the only lawful first-party way
to learn which page earns.

- <https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/help/node/topic/GK5TZZ4AWML2QSLA>

### 3. Decide whether you are racing the 180-day clock (DR-03, P0)

The Associates programme requires three qualifying sales within the first 180 days, and a
rejected application is final. If the clock is short, the site should chase one
high-intent product, not breadth. If it is not, it can build authority across a category.
Those are different sites, and the choice is yours, not researchable.

- <https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/help/node/topic/G8TW5AE9XL2VX9VM>

### 4. Deploy or decline the collector (DR-04, P1)

The privacy-preserving collector is written, tested and off. GitHub Pages cannot run it.
Every "measure and iterate" promise is blocked here, and the campaign ledger is correctly
empty until real first-party reports exist.
