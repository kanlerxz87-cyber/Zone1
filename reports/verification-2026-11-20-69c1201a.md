## 1. The finding that matters commercially

The repository is internally honest and structurally clean, and it currently **cannot earn a
commission**. Both halves of that sentence are verifiable.

Amazon pays only where a purchase follows a click through a *Special Link*, and requires the
Associates tag to be a URL parameter in each such link:

- Program Policies, Participation Requirements §2(b):
  > "All Special Links must be accessed directly from your Site. For example, you must include your
  > Associates ID or 'tag' (appearing as XXXXX-##, or such other format as we may designate) as a
  > parameter in the URL of each link you place on your Site to an Amazon Site."
  — <https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/help/operating/policies>
- Commission Income Statement §1:
  > "We will pay Standard Commission Income … in connection with 'Qualifying Purchases', which …
  > occur when: (a) a customer clicks through a Special Link on your Site to an Amazon Site"
  — same page.

`data/config.json` keeps `associate_tag` empty, so no generated anchor carries a tag. Measured on
the built tree: **711 outbound Amazon/link.amazon anchors, 703 qualified `rel="sponsored"`, 0 tagged.**
The publisher therefore reaches the reader, sends them to a real listing, and records no attribution.

**This is the correct state today** — the tag must not be invented, and the account-holder check in
F76 has not been performed. What was wrong was the *register*: the canonical compliance page had no
row stating that consequence. C14 covered tracking IDs and sub-tags; the parameter requirement itself
existed only in the archived `s2/` annex, which is not the canonical register.

**Repaired in this pass:**

| Change | File |
|---|---|
| New requirement **C19**, status `gated`, quoting §2(b) and the Qualifying Purchase definition | `data/compliance.json` |
| Compliance page renders a `Gated` state and a lede that counts it (18 met + 1 gated) | `build.py`, `compliance.html` |
| New programme flag **F111** recording the register gap and its repair | `build.py`, `flagged.html` |
| Dated re-verification record | `data/sources.json` |

Clearing C19 is a configuration change, not a rewrite: every product anchor already routes through
one builder and is already qualified `sponsored`. The gates that stop a premature tag
(`build.py` `check_decision_data()`) remain in force.
