## What changed in this pass, and why

Two of the five products in the brief are thermometers. Before this pass they had a
dated "link notes" shelf that was explicitly *not* a reader buying guide and was
deliberately excluded from the sitemap. That meant two of five supplied products had no
page a reader — or a search engine — could act on. This pass gives both a real,
manufacturer-sourced buyer page and promotes the pillar to an indexable category.

It also corrects two *published* annex statements that the live record does not support.
Those corrections are the most important finding here, because they are the kind of
error that is invisible unless the source is re-read.
