The important findings

  • The FAFO-001 manual contains contradictory extension-cord passages. That remains a manufacturer clarification, not permission inferred by this site. F96 S15
  • Earlier articles incorrectly said the 8 Qt manual had no aerosol warning and that the oven manual gave no rotisserie limit or internal-temperature chart. Those statements are corrected; automated citation checks had not caught them. F97 S13 S15 S18
  • Publication dates were being inferred from a global source-read date. Unknown first-publication timestamps are now omitted; real edit dates and scoped source reads remain distinct. F98 data/articles.json
  • The old browser and collector did not share one protocol. The repaired version has executable cross-language fixtures and fail-closed deployment gates, not a claimed live endpoint. F99 analytics/README.md

Privacy, cookies & local-only decision storage

This site sets no cookies itself, runs no analytics, and has no public accounts, comment forms or email-signup form. The analytics client is implemented but is not loaded: no production collector is deployed. No production event counts have been measured by this site.

The owner-only decision picker stores choices in this browser’s local storage. It sends nothing automatically; choices leave the page only when explicitly copied or downloaded. Use its clear button to remove the saved choices.

Hosting is separate: GitHub states that Pages logs visitors’ IP addresses for security. Any future analytics host or proxy also needs its own logging and retention review. Following an Amazon link takes you to Amazon, where Amazon’s privacy notice and cookie practices apply.
