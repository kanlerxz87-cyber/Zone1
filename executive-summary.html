2 · Platform capabilities (all implemented as documented workflows)

  • Automated product research — ingest affiliate links, extract ASINs, and pull category, price, rating and availability for trending / high-converting identification.
  • Analysis — pricing, reviews, competition and commission potential, computed from the official category rate table.
  • Content generation — SEO-optimized product articles, comparisons and buying guides, generated only from verified facts.
  • Link management — publish and manage tagged affiliate links; enforce the official Special Link rules.
  • Tracking & optimization — click/conversion tracking and continuous campaign optimization.
  • Compliance — adherence to the Amazon Associates Operating Agreement, Program Policies, and disclosure requirements, sourced from official pages.
