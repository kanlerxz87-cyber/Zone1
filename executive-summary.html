  • Invalid identifiers — ASINs are 10-character codes; these are 9. Sources: ASIN definition, Keywords Everywhere.
  • Invalid link domain — link.amazon is not an official Amazon link/short domain (canonical: amazon.com/dp/<ASIN>; Amazon shorts: amzn.com, amzn.to, a.co).
  • Broken resolution — each supplied URL falls through Amazon's missing-page fallback (plpRedirect=mhFallback, lv=shuf) to a different random unrelated listing.
Consequence (no-hallucination policy): product title, price, rating, review count, category, competition, commission rate and niche are blank and flagged unverified everywhere. The marketing content is fully built and compliant but makes no product claims until valid data exists. A parallel session's contrary identity mapping is preserved but disputed (flag F17).
