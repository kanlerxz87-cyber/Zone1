3. Key findings

  1. The three links are a single-category, two-brand programme. 100% of the links are air fryers and 2 of 3 are Chefman — 3 of 3 sit in Amazon's Home & Kitchen category. Concentration risk is the dominant structural finding (see also I-2).
  2. The programme cannot currently earn. Zero of the three links carry a tracking tag, so zero of the three can be verified as a commission-bearing Special Link. This is the single highest-priority fix (I-2).
  3. The three products are genuinely different machines, not variants. 6 Qt dual-basket, 26 Qt French-door oven, 8 Qt single-basket XL — a real three-way buying-guide story exists without any invented claims.
  4. The weakest verified signal is the 26 Qt oven's own review aggregate. The manufacturer's own page shows 2.6/5 from 25 reviews (source). Any content that recommends it must address that honestly rather than bury it.
  5. Amazon's public data surface is much smaller than the brief assumed. Prices, star ratings, review counts, BSR and the category commission rate all require authentication or API access this account does not yet have. The site is therefore built to be honest about gaps rather than to look complete.
  6. The 2026 policy change materially constrains paid acquisition. Purchases referred through paid or boosted ads that link to Amazon are now excluded from commission, with limited exceptions (C22). The growth plan is therefore content/SEO-led, not ad-led.
