The three real differences

  1. Workflow, not size. The 6 Qt splits into two independently-controlled 3 Qt baskets with Sync Finish; the 8 Qt gives the whole 8 Qt to one thing. The dual-basket wins on "main plus side"; the single basket wins on "a lot of one thing".
  2. 450 °F versus 400 °F. The 8 Qt has a dedicated Hi-Fry button that lifts the temperature to 450 °F / 232 °C for the final two minutes. The 6 Qt dual-basket tops out at 400 °F / 204 °C. If maximum browning is the goal, that is the deciding spec.
  3. Review depth on the brand's own site. The 8 Qt page carries 200 reviews against the 6 Qt's 30 — thin either way, and neither is an Amazon rating. Amazon ratings could not be captured (I-11).
