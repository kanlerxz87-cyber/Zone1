Why no niche is named as profitable

Naming a “profitable niche” requires demand, competition, price, review and commission inputs for that niche. None of the five can be read from a public official source for a specific product family without an account, and a list of niches invented from general knowledge would be exactly the kind of unverified claim this brief excludes. The rubric is therefore published complete and the ranking empty.
