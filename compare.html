The three real differences

Form factor

Two 3 qt-class drawers, one 8 qt basket, or a 26 qt oven cavity. This decides what you can cook simultaneously far more than raw litres do.

Documented ceiling

450 °F (Chefman 6 Qt, per S10), 400 °F (Chefman 8 Qt, per S12), 500 °F (Emeril, per S15). The 8 Qt's listing claims 450 °F — see the conflict note.

Information quality

The Chefman 6 Qt publishes the most complete spec sheet, and its owner manual settles the questions the product page left open. The Chefman 8 Qt still has one unresolved contradiction between the listing and its own manual, logged on the flagged page.

Decision rules

  1. Cooking two different things at once, on a normal counter? The dual-basket unit is the only one designed for that.
  2. Feeding four to six people from one basket? The 8 qt single basket is the documented "4–6 people" unit S12.
  3. Replacing a toaster oven as well? Only the 26 qt oven-style unit does rotisserie and toast, and it is the one whose footprint you should measure first (16.9 × 15.8 × 14.8 in S14).
  4. Worried about the 450 °F claim on the 8 qt? Read the manual that ships with the unit. This site will not resolve that conflict for you by guessing.
