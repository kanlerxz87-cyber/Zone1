Four clusters, 24 articles

A small site earns a query by answering it completely rather than by publishing one page per idea. This is the shape of the set: the four questions a shopper brings here, the hub that answers each one, and the articles underneath it. Every link on this page is generated from data/clusters.json, so the map and the articles cannot drift apart.

Why this is not a taxonomy exercise

The clusters are not tags. Each one is a question a reader arrives with, and each article is filed under the question it actually answers. A piece about cleaning sits with ownership questions, not with the format comparisons, even when it mentions the same three products.

Privacy, cookies & local-only decision storage
