## 4. NEW FINDING — the Chefman 6 Qt and 8 Qt model mapping is narrower than published (F32)

This is the finding this pass adds. It does not change a published value, but it changes what the
site's model-mapping flags mean, and it must be published.

`data/products.json` records product 1's model as **RJ38-SQPF-3TDB-2W**, justified by a match on
capacity, finish and brand wording, and it notes the sister SKU **RJ38-SQPF-3TDB-2**. Both of those
pages were confirmed live. But Chefman's own store carries **three further 6-quart dual-basket SKUs**
in the same family, and this session read all of them live:

| Model (from the page's own Specification block) | Product name on chefman.com | Temperature range | Stated functions/presets | On-site rating |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `RJ38-3DBW-V2` | TurboFry 6 Qt. Double Basket Air Fryer with Easy View Windows | `90–400°F / 32–204°C` | `5 Cooking Functions: Air fry, bake, reheat, dehydrate, and frozen` | 4.5 from 30 |
| `RJ38-SQPF-3TDB-2` | TurboFry® Touch 6 Qt. Dual-Basket Air Fryer | (no spec block published on this page) | (none published) | 4.6 from 194 |
| `RJ38-SQPF-3TDB-2W` | TurboFry® Touch 6 Qt. Dual-Window Air Fryer | `200–450°F / 95º-235ºC` | Hi-Fry to 450 °F, Sync Finish, Sync Baskets | 4.6 from 1,305 |

The Amazon title for ASIN B0CDY277D3 says `Hi-Fry` and `Easy View Windows`. `Hi-Fry` is documented
only on the **`-2W`** page (450 °F). Easy View windows are documented on **all three**. So the title
is consistent with `-2W`, and the stored mapping stands — but the field is **not** settled by the
title alone, and the site must say that three same-capacity SKUs exist.

**Related, and more consequential (F33).** For product 3 the site publishes `Hi-Fry / 450 °F mode:
None` and explains it as a conflict between the Amazon title (450 °F Hi-Fry, Bake/Dehydrate/Frozen)
and the brand page for `RJ38-SQSS-8T` (200–400 °F, four food presets). This session found that
Chefman sells a **different 8-quart SKU that matches the Amazon title exactly**:

| Model | Product name | Hi-Fry | Functions | Colour |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `RJ38-SQSS-8T` (stored mapping) | TurboFry® Touch **Stainless-Steel** Air Fryer | — (max 400 °F) | 4 food presets | Stainless steel |
| `RJ38-8T-BLACK` | TurboFry 8 Qt. Air Fryer **w/ High Fry** | **450 °F for the final two minutes** | **4 functions: air fry, bake, dehydrate, frozen** | **Matte black** |

The Amazon title for ASIN B08DKYBTPH reads `… 4-in-1 with 450°F Hi-Fry Option … | Bake, Dehydrate,
Frozen | … Stainless Steel`. The *function list* and the *450 °F Hi-Fry* match `RJ38-8T-BLACK`
character for character; the *colour* matches `RJ38-SQSS-8T`. So **the two official sources are not
necessarily in conflict** — they may simply describe two different SKUs, and the listing may be a
third variant or may mislabel its finish. This **narrows** flag F3 substantially and gives the
account holder a concrete action (open the listing and read the model plate / the "Product
information" model number). It does **not** resolve it, and this pass asserts neither model.
