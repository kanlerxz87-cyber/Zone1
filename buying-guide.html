Buying guide: how to choose the right product, whatever you're shopping for

A category-agnostic buying framework — the same method the platform's generated guides use. It makes no claims about specific products (the three submitted links resolve to none — see Products), and it applies equally once real products are attached.

1 · Start with the job, not the product

Write one sentence: “I need this to ___.” Every criterion below traces back to that sentence. Shopping without it is how people overpay for features they'll never use.

2 · The five criteria that actually matter

3 · Read reviews like an analyst

  • Weight 3–4★ reviews most — they usually list pros and cons.
  • Sort by most recent; products change between production runs.
  • Treat patterns as signal (“12 reviews mention the same failure”) and single stories as noise.
  • Be skeptical of extreme praise with no detail — and of reviews that describe a different product.

4 · Budget bands (a practical rule)

  • Budget — covers the core job only; expect trade-offs in materials/finish.
  • Mid-range — the sweet spot for most buyers: full fit + solid quality.
  • Premium — pay for durability, support, or a specific premium feature you named in step 1.

5 · Timing and deals

Check whether the item is seasonal for its category, set a price-watch instead of impulse-buying, and remember that “list price” is not the same as what you'll pay — look at the actual checkout price at the time of purchase.
