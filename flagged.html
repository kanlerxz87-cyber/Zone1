F-01 medium

The three short links resolve via a redirect handler that carries a fallback marker

What was expected
A short link that resolves cleanly to the exact product originally intended.
What was observed
All three link.amazon short links redirect to amazon.com/dp/<ASIN> but the resolved URL carries plpRedirect=mhFallback. The short codes (B04G1MEwY, B02NbDxp3, B00hJ57DV) are opaque and contain no ASIN, so the ASIN mapping can only be established from the redirect target.
Why it matters
If a short link has expired or been re-pointed, the ASIN it now opens may not be the product the link was created for. Commission would then be earned on a product the operator did not choose.
What would settle it
The operator re-checks each short link in a normal browser, or replaces the short links with the canonical tagged /dp/<ASIN> URLs.

Evidence: B0CDY277D3 listing ↗ B09B7SB46R listing ↗ B08DKYBTPH listing ↗

F-02 medium

“Hi-Fry” appears in the Amazon title for B0CDY277D3 but not in Chefman's specification for the matching model

What was expected
Every named feature in a retail title to appear in the manufacturer's own feature list.
What was observed
Amazon title names “Hi-Fry”. Chefman's page for RJ38-3DBW-V2 lists five functions (air fry, bake, reheat, dehydrate, frozen) over 90–400°F and does not list “Hi-Fry”. Chefman's separate 8 Qt model RJ38-8T-BLACK does list “Hi-Fry” at 450°F — so the term exists in the brand's vocabulary, just not for this model.
Why it matters
Publishing “Hi-Fry” as a feature of this unit would be an unsupported product claim (see C-14). It may be a title error, or the Amazon SKU may be a variant the brand page does not cover.
What would settle it
Chefman support confirmation of the model number on the Amazon listing, or the owner manual for the exact Amazon SKU.

Evidence: Chefman RJ38-3DBW-V2 page ↗ Amazon B0CDY277D3 ↗

F-08 high

Amazon price, star rating and review count are blank by design

What was expected
Live Amazon metrics for the comparison table.
What was observed
The Amazon product pages served an interstitial instead of price and rating blocks to this client. Separately, Amazon's own Program Policies §2(b) (§2(b)) only permit showing prices and availability if Amazon serves the data or it comes through the Creators API / PA API, and §3(t) (§3(t)) restricts displaying Amazon reviews and star ratings to API-sourced data.
Why it matters
These are the three numbers a buyer most wants, and a converter most wants. They are blank because they cannot be lawfully displayed from this crawl, not because they are unavailable in principle.
What would settle it
Obtaining Creators API / PA API access and rendering Amazon-served widgets. Until then the correct action is to leave the cells empty, which is what the site does.

Evidence: Amazon Program Policies §2(b) and §3(t) ↗

F-10 high

No Amazon Associate tag has been supplied, so no link on this site can currently earn

What was expected
Each outbound link carrying the operator's Associates tag.
What was observed
No Associates tag was supplied with this project. Amazon requires the tag as a URL parameter on every Special Link.
Why it matters
Untagged links are not Special Links and generate no commission. This is the single highest-impact blocker between this site and revenue.
What would settle it
The operator adds their tag to data/config.json (format XXXXX-20) and the build re-renders every outbound link. The build script validates the tag and refuses to emit a malformed one.

Evidence: Amazon Program Policies §2(b) ↗ Amazon Operating Agreement §1 ↗

F-11 medium

Click and conversion tracking is architected but deliberately not shipped

What was expected
The brief asks for click and conversion tracking.
What was observed
Conversion data lives only in the Associates dashboard; Amazon states it uses commercially reasonable efforts to track Qualifying Purchases and reports them monthly. There is no public API for per-click conversion attribution to a third-party static site. Meanwhile §3(g), §3(v) and §3(e) constrain how tracking may be done.
Why it matters
A static GitHub Pages site cannot truthfully report conversions. Shipping a fake or inferred conversion number would breach the no-hallucination rule.
What would settle it
Build the outbound-click counter server-side (no cloaking, no incentives, no cookies on the static site) and read conversions from the Associates dashboard, not from a scraped or estimated source.

Evidence: Amazon Program Policies ↗ Commission Income Statement §6 (reporting) ↗

F-12 low

Review-rating comparison mixes brand-store review systems, not Amazon

What was expected
A like-for-like review metric.
What was observed
Chefman's pages report 4.5/5 (30 reviews) and 4.6/5 (200 reviews) through their own Bazaarvoice/Wholescale widget; Emeril Everyday reports 2.6/5 (25 reviews) through Bazaarvoice. These are three brand storefronts with different volumes and different moderation, not one marketplace.
Why it matters
The Emeril 2.6 vs Chefman 4.5 gap is real on those sources, but a small, low-volume brand-store sample is not statistically comparable to a marketplace rating. The site labels every rating as “brand-store” and adds this caveat rather than presenting it as a verdict.
What would settle it
Amazon star ratings via the Creators API, compared at equal volume.

Evidence: Chefman 6 Qt page ↗ Chefman 8 Qt page ↗ Emeril Everyday page ↗

F-13 medium

No search-volume, sales-rank or conversion-rate data exists from an official source

What was expected
Demand and competition metrics for niche scoring.
What was observed
Amazon publishes no sales volume, search volume or conversion rate for individual ASINs. Third-party tools that estimate these (Jungle Scout, Helium 10 and similar) are commercial estimates, not official sources, and their numbers are not reproducible.
Why it matters
The brief asks for data-driven niche scoring on trending and high-converting products. Any scoring that produces a confident number would be fabricated.
What would settle it
Use Amazon's own reports where available (e.g. Bounty/Bonus event tables, which ARE published) plus the operator's own observed click-through and conversion from the Associates dashboard once live. Until then, the opportunity score on this site is computed only from factors that ARE sourced, and the unsourced dimensions are shown as blank with this flag.

Evidence: Amazon Standard Commission Income Statement (published data) ↗
